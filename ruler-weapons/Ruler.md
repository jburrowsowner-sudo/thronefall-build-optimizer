## Craaghelm

| Ruler stat | Without upgrades or perks |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum HP | 50 HP |
| Self-healing | 5 HP/s after 1 s without losing HP |
| Night movement: walk / sprint | 14 / 23 units/s |
| Day movement: walk / sprint | 18.2 / 29.9 units/s |
| While knocked out | 6 units/s; revive after 11 s at night (50 HP at base stats) |
| Loadout | 1 weapon; up to 5 perks |

The dwarf ruler normally needs full HP to sprint. Dawn revives a fallen ruler without waiting for the timer. Damage reductions from separate sources add together and cannot reduce a hit below zero.

**Weapon choices:** [Core Thumper (Craaghelm)](Core%20Thumper.md), [Dragon’s Roar (Craaghelm)](Dragon%E2%80%99s%20Roar.md), [Dwarven Axe (Craaghelm)](Dwarven%20Axe.md), [Keg and Hammer (Craaghelm)](Keg%20and%20Hammer.md), [Royal Spanner (Craaghelm)](Royal%20Spanner.md), [Stormcoil (Craaghelm)](Stormcoil.md), [Twin Bastions (Craaghelm)](Twin%20Bastions.md).
