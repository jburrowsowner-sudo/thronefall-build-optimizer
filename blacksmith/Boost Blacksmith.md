# Boost Blacksmith (Research boost, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Spend [Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](../economy/Energy%20Cores.md) during the day to subtract **1 remaining research day** from a current [Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Blacksmith.md#craaghelm) project. It can finish immediately; no need to wait for the next dawn. Each project accepts one boost, even before the building reaches level III.

## Affects

**once per active project**

| Affects | Stat | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Melee Armor (Craaghelm)](Melee%20Armor.md#craaghelm) · [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm) · [Ranged Armor (Craaghelm)](Ranged%20Armor.md#craaghelm) · [Ranged Attack (Craaghelm)](Ranged%20Attack.md#craaghelm) | Remaining research time | −1 day |
