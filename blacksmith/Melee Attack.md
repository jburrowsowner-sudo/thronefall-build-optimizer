# Melee Attack (Blacksmith)

**Melee damage multiplier: 1.25** per completed selection. Requires 3 successful dawns, or 2 with Faster Research.

Applies to Knights, Spearmen, Flails, Berserk strikes and landings, Hunter knives, Golems and Lizard Rider bites. Also affects tower oil, barricades, wall and gate explosions, mill traps, melee ruler attacks, Explosive Revival and War Horse damage and recoil.

## Craaghelm

Available at [Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Blacksmith.md#craaghelm) I, II or III. **Melee-channel damage multiplier: 1.25** per completed pick. Requires **3 successful dawns** by default. The multiplier after one / two / three completions is **1.25 / 1.56 / 1.95**.

This follows the attack, not the unit’s name. It includes both attacks of [Axe Throwers (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Axe%20Throwers.md) and [Ancestor (Craaghelm)](../ancestral-workshop/Ancestor.md), the [Bootleggers (Craaghelm)](../barracks/Bootleggers.md) strike (not the heal), the [Mad Berserker (Craaghelm)](../ancestral-workshop/Mad%20Berserker.md) strike (not the execution), and [Explosive Brew (Craaghelm)](../mills/Explosive%20Brew.md).

[Boost Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Blacksmith.md) can shorten an active project on maps with Energy Cores.
