# Melee Armor (Blacksmith)

**Incoming melee damage multiplier: 0.76** per completed selection. Requires 2 successful dawns, or 1 with Faster Research.

Includes Ram hits, Exploder and Fire Snail death blasts, and Pikes’ weapon hits. Excludes Pikes’ damage on entering reach and War Horse recoil.

**Enemy attacks:** Barrel Knight, Big Spider, Dark Warrior, Exploder, Fire Snail, Flail, Flogre, Ghost, Hunterling, Mole Warrior, Monster Rider, Ogre, Peasant, Pikes, Racer, Ram, Slime, Small Spider, Spider, Spiky Slime, Swordsman, Thing, Wheel.

## Craaghelm

Available at [Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Blacksmith.md#craaghelm) I, II or III. **Incoming melee-channel damage multiplier: 0.76** per completed pick. Requires **2 successful dawns** by default. The multiplier after one / two / three completions is **0.76 / 0.59 / 0.45**.

An attack must be flagged for melee resistance. The divisor follows the incoming weapon, not whether the victim fights at close range.

[Boost Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Blacksmith.md) can shorten an active project on maps with Energy Cores.
