# Ranged Attack (Blacksmith)

**Ranged damage multiplier: 1.2** per completed selection. Requires 3 successful dawns, or 2 with Faster Research.

Applies to archers, Hunter arrows, Firewing, Lizard Rider snipes, ranged ruler attacks, tower shots, the castle, shrines, Fortified Houses, Wind Spirits and Scarecrows. Includes Fire Archer ground fire and Fire Spire’s projectile impact, but not its oil or Healing Spire shots.

## Craaghelm

Available at [Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Blacksmith.md#craaghelm) I, II or III. **Ranged-channel damage multiplier: 1.2** per completed pick. Requires **3 successful dawns** by default. The multiplier after one / two / three completions is **1.2 / 1.44 / 1.72**.

Applies to matching shots and blasts, including [Grenadiers (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Grenadiers.md), [Technician (Craaghelm)](../heroes-quarters/Technician.md) drones, [Railgun Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Railgun%20Tower.md), and [Draft Winds (Craaghelm)](../mills/Draft%20Winds.md). [Siege Engineer’s Turret (Craaghelm)](../heroes-quarters/Siege%20Engineer%E2%80%99s%20Turret.md) bolts and [Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](../towers/Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm) healing do not use this channel.

[Boost Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Blacksmith.md) can shorten an active project on maps with Energy Cores.
