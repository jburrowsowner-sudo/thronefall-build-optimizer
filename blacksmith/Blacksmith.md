# Blacksmith (Blacksmith)

Choose melee damage, ranged damage, melee armor or ranged armor at each tier.

80 HP and no income. Each of the 3 research tiers requires the matching Town Center tier. Repeated selections multiply.

Research advances at dawn unless the Blacksmith was knocked out that night.

## Craaghelm

This **80-HP** building has three research levels. Select one project each time; choices can repeat and completed factors multiply. A project gains one day at each dawn after a night the building survived. New research waits until the current project finishes.

| Research | Per completed pick | Dawns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm) | Melee-channel damage: 1.25 | 3 |
| [Ranged Attack (Craaghelm)](Ranged%20Attack.md#craaghelm) | Ranged-channel damage: 1.2 | 3 |
| [Melee Armor (Craaghelm)](Melee%20Armor.md#craaghelm) | Incoming melee-channel damage: 0.76 | 2 |
| [Ranged Armor (Craaghelm)](Ranged%20Armor.md#craaghelm) | Incoming ranged-channel damage: 0.76 | 2 |

[Boost Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Blacksmith.md) removes one day from a project already underway.
