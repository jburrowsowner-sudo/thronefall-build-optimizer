# Absolute Order (Town Center 2, Craaghelm)

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) II · **+200 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

While the ruler locks a target at night, supported troop and hero attacks prioritize that target if eligible and multiply their cooldown by **0.5**. The order checks the lock every 0.5 s; releasing it restores the interval. This does not turn a repair or heal into an attack.
