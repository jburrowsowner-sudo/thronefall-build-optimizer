## Craaghelm

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) III · **+400 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

Reduces the Mithril needed to improve eligible towers, walls and gates. Also fully heals the ruler and multiplies maximum health by **1.4** and self-regeneration by **1.4**.
