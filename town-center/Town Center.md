# Town Center (Town Center)

Choose one permanent upgrade at each of tiers II, III and IV. All branches are unlocked on this map.

| Tier | HP | Shot interval (s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 100 | 4 |
| II | 300 | 2 |
| III | 700 | 1 |
| IV | 1700 | 1 |

One arrow per shot; range **35**, damage **4.5** or **2.7** against ranged armor. Shot interval multiplier: 0.9–1.1. The castle gains ranged damage bonuses, but not defense tower bonuses.

**Town Center arrow:** projectile speed 30 units/s; maximum chase distance 10,000.

## Craaghelm

**Craaghelm name:** Castle Center

The castle starts at **100 HP** and fires at range **35** for **4.5** base damage, or **2.7** against ranged armor. It reaches level III here, not IV.

| Level | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| II | [Absolute Order (Craaghelm)](Absolute%20Order.md), [Tower Creator (Craaghelm)](Tower%20Creator.md), [Dwarven Nobility (Craaghelm)](Dwarven%20Nobility.md), [Builder's Guild (Craaghelm)](Builder's%20Guild.md#craaghelm) |
| III | [Tower Order (Craaghelm)](Tower%20Order.md), [Blessing (Craaghelm)](Blessing.md), [Castle-Up (Craaghelm)](Castle-Up.md#craaghelm), [Commander (Craaghelm)](Commander.md#craaghelm) |

| Path | Castle health | Nominal firing intervals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 100 | 4 s |
| II: Tower Creator | 500 | 1 s and 4 s |
| II: other choice | 300 | 2 s |
| III: Commander after Tower Creator / other | 700 / 500 | 1 s and 2 s / 2 s |
| III: other choice after Tower Creator / other | 900 / 700 | 1 s and 4 s / 1 s |

Two intervals mean two independent attacks, not a single shot with a faster cooldown.
