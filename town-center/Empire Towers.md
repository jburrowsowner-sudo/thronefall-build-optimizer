# Empire Towers (Town Center 4)

Town Center IV · +1000 castle HP.

**Tower output multiplier: 1.5** for shot damage, Healing Spire healing and Fire Spire oil damage. Also applies to towers built later.

At dusk, outgoing melee and ranged damage use **1 + gold / 1,500**; incoming damage uses **1,500 / (1,500 + gold)**. Gold is not spent.
