# Dwarven Nobility (Town Center 2, Craaghelm)

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) II · **+200 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

The ruler heals to full. Maximum health and ruler-scaled damage are multiplied by **1.6** and **1.6** respectively. Night movement speed is multiplied by **1.3**; daytime speed and self-regeneration rate do not change.
