# Tower Creator (Town Center 2, Craaghelm)

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) II · **+400 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

Tower target-search ranges are multiplied by **1.3**, and supported tower attack amounts by **1.5**. This changes neither the physical reach of a flame line nor a weapon’s blast radius. The castle itself also gains a second, faster shot.
