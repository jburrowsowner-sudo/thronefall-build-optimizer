# Builder's Guild (Town Center 2)

Town Center II · +200 castle HP.

**Ruler multipliers:** max HP, self-healing rate: 1.5. Refills a living ruler.

On purchase and each early dusk, upgrade the nearest eligible tier I House to tier II. A normal House gains 1 gold per harvest.

## Impacted damage dealers

- **Max HP:** [Blood Wand](../ruler-weapons/Blood%20Wand.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Self-healing rate:** [Falchion & Traps](../ruler-weapons/Falchion%20%26%20Traps.md) · [Light Spear](../ruler-weapons/Light%20Spear.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Current HP:** [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **House upgrade:** [House](../economy/House.md)
- **Castle HP:** [Town Center](Town%20Center.md)

## Craaghelm

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) II · **+200 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

Immediately upgrades the nearest eligible level I House, then repeats once each dusk. It can pick [House (Craaghelm)](../economy/House.md#craaghelm) or the House-tagged [Energy Pole (Craaghelm)](../economy/Energy%20Pole.md); if neither can upgrade, nothing happens. The ruler also heals to full and multiplies both max HP and self-regeneration by **1.5**.
