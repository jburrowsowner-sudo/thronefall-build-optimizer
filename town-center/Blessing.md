# Blessing (Town Center 3, Craaghelm)

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) III · **+400 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

The first time a supported tower falls, it revives after **0.1 s** at **0.5 of max HP**. The tower suppresses its death-spawn effect until this revive. The revive counter does not reset at dawn.
