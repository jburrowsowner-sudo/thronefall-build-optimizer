# Tower Order (Town Center 3, Craaghelm)

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) III · **+400 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

While the ruler locks an enemy at night, eligible towers prioritize that target and multiply their attack cooldown by **0.5**. They check the lock every 0.5 s. Range and target restrictions still apply; a [Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](../towers/Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm) does not shoot an enemy.
