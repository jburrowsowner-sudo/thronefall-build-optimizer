# Godly Curse (Town Center 3)

Town Center III · +400 castle HP.

**Ruler multipliers:** max HP, self-healing rate: 1.75. Refills a living ruler.

The first qualifying hit on each target per night deals an extra **0.25 of its max HP**. Applies to sword hits, spear thrusts, axe strikes, bow shots and stabs, falchion strikes and traps, lightning strikes, Blood Wand hits, the initial Shadow Codex mark and the Potion Vials active. Excludes Codex spread and blast, passive vial attacks and War Horse collisions. Damage bonuses do not multiply the extra hit.
