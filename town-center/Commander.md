# Commander (Town Center 3)

Town Center III · +400 castle HP.

Following troops and heroes retain their attacks; movement speed multiplier **1.6**. When holding position, the HP multiplier is **1.2** and the attack strength multiplier is **1.2**, including Support Mage healing. A following Berserk cannot start a leap.

## Craaghelm

[Castle Center (Craaghelm)](Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) III · **+200 castle HP**. Choose one upgrade at this tier.

Commanded units can attack while following; their movement speed is multiplied by **1.6**. On hold position, their health and own attack amounts are multiplied by **1.2 / 1.2**. The castle gains less HP than with the other tier III choices.
