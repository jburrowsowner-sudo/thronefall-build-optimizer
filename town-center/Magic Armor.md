# Magic Armor (Town Center 2)

Town Center II · +200 castle HP.

**Ruler multipliers:** max HP: 2.5; self-healing rate: 2. Refills a living ruler.

When a weapon hit removes ruler HP, retaliate for **7 damage** against a ranged attacker or **1** against another attacker. Blacksmith and ruler damage bonuses do not scale retaliation.

- **Max HP:** [Blood Wand](../ruler-weapons/Blood%20Wand.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Self-healing rate:** [Falchion & Traps](../ruler-weapons/Falchion%20%26%20Traps.md) · [Light Spear](../ruler-weapons/Light%20Spear.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Current HP, Retaliation damage:** [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Castle HP:** [Town Center](Town%20Center.md)
