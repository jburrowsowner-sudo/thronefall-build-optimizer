# Spawn lanes (Wave)

| Lane | Eligible enemies |
| --- | --- |
| Left Air | Wasp, Fury, Flying Mage, Bloodwing, Wind-Boat |
| Left Bridge | Ground and flying enemies |
| Right Air | Wasp, Fury, Flying Mage, Bloodwing, Wind-Boat |
| Middle Bridge | Ground and flying enemies |
| Right Bridge | Ground and flying enemies |

All groups share one bridge on even nights; air lanes are used only on odd nights.
