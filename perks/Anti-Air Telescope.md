# Anti-Air Telescope (Perk)

## Effects

**Stat:** Max HP, Auto-attack damage

**Multiplier:** 0.75 · **Target:** Flying enemies

## Hidden mechanics

It weakens flying enemies when they spawn; it does not strengthen your archers. Their regular attacks lose damage, but a separate ability does not automatically inherit that reduction.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Bloodwing](../enemies/Bloodwing.md) · [Flying Mage](../enemies/Flying%20Mage.md) · [Fury](../enemies/Fury.md) · [Wasp](../enemies/Wasp.md) · [Wind-Boat](../enemies/Wind-Boat.md)

## Craaghelm

Weakens the **enemy at spawn**, not the ally shooting it. The Gyro Blade (Brewerk onward) and Quarry Drone (Schattengrund onward) enter with 0.75 of normal max HP and 0.75 of normal automatic-attack damage. Widerhall and Myrkforst offer the perk but have no matching campaign flyer.

The Airship is a player unit, and Fafnir the Awoken is not tagged as flying. Neither is weakened.
