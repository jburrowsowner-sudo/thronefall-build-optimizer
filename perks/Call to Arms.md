# Call to Arms (Perk, Craaghelm)

A standing recruitment building replaces a fallen recruit at **1.6 times normal training speed**. The returning unit moves at 1.3 times normal speed for 15 s. A dawn or Ring of Resurrection revival does not grant the sprint.

| Recruitment branch | Normal replacement | With this perk alone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary ranged or melee, tier I / II / III | 10 / 6 / 6 s | 6.25 / 3.75 / 3.75 s |
| Each of the four hero choices | 60 s | 37.5 s |
| Either Ancestral Workshop choice | 20 s | 12.5 s |

The building must be standing and have an active recruit down. Its training boost multiplies this rate rather than subtracting a fixed time. The sprint multiplies Veteran movement; it does not speed attacks.
