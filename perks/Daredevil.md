## Hidden mechanics

The damage bonus grows smoothly with the fraction of HP missing; there is no low-health threshold. Healing reduces it again. It applies to attacks that use ruler damage, including War Horse recoil, but not the Explosive Revival blast.

## Craaghelm

Missing ruler health raises the damage of all seven Craaghelm weapon choices. The multiplier is **1 + 1.15 times the missing HP fraction**: 1 at full health, 1.57 at half health and a limit of 2.15 as HP approaches zero.

This affects damaging hits, including shield release, charge finishers and the Royal Spanner’s enemy damage—not its healing. A timed keg reads ruler damage when its attack timer first initializes, not afresh for each enemy in the blast.
