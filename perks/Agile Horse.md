# Agile Horse (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sprint | Available below full HP | Ruler |

## Hidden mechanics

Taking damage no longer forces the ruler to wait for full health before sprinting. The perk changes when you can sprint, not how fast you move; enemy slows still apply.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)

## Craaghelm

**Craaghelm name:** Agile Boar

The boar can sprint while the ruler is wounded. Its ordinary speeds do not increase: 23 units/s sprinting at night and 29.9 by day before other effects. Slows still apply; this perk removes the full-health check, not the slow.
