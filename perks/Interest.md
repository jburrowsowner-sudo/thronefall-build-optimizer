# Interest (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | +min(20, ⌈purse / 3⌉) | At dusk | Ruler |

## Hidden mechanics

Only gold already in your purse at dusk counts. The payout rounds up, so 58 gold is enough for the full 20; coins still on the ground do not count.

## Impacted damage dealers

None directly.
