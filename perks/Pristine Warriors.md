# Pristine Warriors (Perk)

## Effects

**Stat:** Max HP, Attack damage

**Multiplier:** 1.75 · **Target:** Barracks troops

## Hidden mechanics

It strengthens both the Berserk’s regular strike and its landing hit. The Golem does not qualify just because it fights in melee; only Barracks recruits get the bonus.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Berserks](../barracks/Berserks.md) · [Flails](../barracks/Flails.md) · [Knights](../barracks/Knights.md) · [Spearmen](../barracks/Spearmen.md)

## Craaghelm

Soldiers, Axe Wielders, Bootleggers and Shield Bearers multiply **attack strength by 1.75**. The Bootlegger’s healing shot and melee hit both benefit. An Automaton or Ancestral Workshop fighter does not.

None of the four playable Barracks recruits gains max HP. The pristine health bonus on the unused basic melee template does not transfer to them.
