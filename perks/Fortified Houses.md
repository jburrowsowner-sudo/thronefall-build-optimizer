## Hidden mechanics

Tier II replaces the tier I attack instead of adding a second archer. Tier III keeps that same attack interval. The arrows use ranged damage research and hit ranged-armored enemies for less.

## Craaghelm

Houses gain **3.5 times their normal HP** and shoot at enemies within 34 units. Energy Poles also get the HP multiplier, but their live upgrades never switch on either house gun.

| House tier | Base shot damage | Nominal interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 8 | 5 s |
| II | 8 | 1.66 s |

The tier II gun replaces the tier I gun. Each shot uses ranged research; ranged armor multiplies it by 0.6, ranged vulnerability by 1.7. Resilient Residences multiplies the HP again.
