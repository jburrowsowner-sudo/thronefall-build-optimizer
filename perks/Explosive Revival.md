# Explosive Revival (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revival blast damage | 90 HP | Nearby enemies |
| Blast radius | 8.5 units | Ruler position |

**Condition:** Timed ruler revival

## Hidden mechanics

The blast fires when the ruler’s revival timer ends, including a quick Ring of Resurrection revival. Returning at dawn does not trigger it. Melee Attack strengthens the blast; Glass Cannon and Daredevil do not.
