# Stronger Heroes (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max HP | 1.5 | All four heroes |
| Attack damage | 1.4 | Golem, Firewing, Lizard Rider |
| Healing per orb | 1.4 | Support Mage |

## Hidden mechanics

Attack strength includes the Support Mage’s healing orbs and both the Lizard Rider’s arrow and bite. It applies to recruited heroes, not the ruler.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Firewing](../heroes-quarters/Firewing.md) · [Golem](../heroes-quarters/Golem.md) · [Lizard Rider](../heroes-quarters/Lizard%20Rider.md)

## Craaghelm

Automaton, Airship, Technician, Siege Engineer, Ancestor and Mad Berserker multiply **max HP by 1.5** and **automatic attack strength by 1.4**. The last two qualify despite coming from the Ancestral Workshop.

Technician drone hits, the engineer’s repair pulses and both Ancestor weapons benefit. The Engineer Turret has its own HP and crossbow and does not inherit the hero’s multipliers. Nor do ordinary troops, the Spectral Archer or the ruler.
