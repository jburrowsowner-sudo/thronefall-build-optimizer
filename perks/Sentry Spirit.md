# Sentry Spirit (Perk, Craaghelm)

A fallen Defense Tower releases one **Spectral Archer** at the tower for up to **30 s**. Reviving the tower dismisses its archer immediately. With the Castle Center’s Blessing active, the first knockout is reserved for the tower’s revival; a later knockout can release the archer.

| Spectral Archer, before bonuses | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Movement / enemy targeting | Stationary; not registered as a target |
| Shot / against ranged armor | 7 / 4.2 damage |
| Range / nominal shot interval | 20 / 1 s (0.9–1.1 s) |

Its arrow can hit ground or flying enemies, but not hidden targets. Its attack is active; its HP, tag registration and movement are disabled. Pristine Archers and Firearm Drills improve its shooting. It cannot be healed, commanded or trained back into battle.
