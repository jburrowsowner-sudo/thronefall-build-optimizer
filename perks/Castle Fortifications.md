# Castle Fortifications (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max HP | 2.5 | — |
| Arrow range | 1.5 | — |
| Attack interval | 0.33 | — |
| Target recheck interval | 0.33 | No target |

**Target:** Town Center

## Hidden mechanics

The Town Center fires sooner and rechecks for targets sooner when none are in range. Damage per arrow does not change. Tier IV keeps the tier III attack.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Town Center](../town-center/Town%20Center.md)

## Craaghelm

The **Castle Center** has 2.5 times normal max HP, including its branch HP gains. The gun selected by its upgrades acquires targets out to 52.5 instead of 35; it does not hit harder.

| Enabled gun | Normal interval | Fortified interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 4 s | 1.32 s |
| 2 | 2 s | 0.66 s |
| 3 | 1 s | 0.33 s |

The empty-range recheck is also multiplied by 0.33. These are gun profiles, not an assumption that every upgrade branch fires the next gun.
