# Gods' Lotion (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regeneration | 2.1 | — |
| Regeneration delay | 0.5 | After taking damage |

**Target:** Ruler

## Hidden mechanics

With no other bonuses, the ruler begins regenerating after 0.5 s without losing HP and recovers 10.5 HP/s. Healing Gold also uses this regeneration bonus, but Battle Axe lifesteal does not.
