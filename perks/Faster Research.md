# Faster Research (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Research time | Blacksmith and Royal Forge |
| Research time | Blacksmith |
| Research time | Royal Forge |

**Value:** −1 dawn · **Condition:** New research

## Hidden mechanics

Research still advances at dawn, not when you select it. A knockout can still cancel that dawn’s progress unless you also have Relentless Research. Reloading does not shorten a running timer again.

## Impacted damage dealers

None directly.
