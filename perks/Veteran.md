# Veteran (Perk, Craaghelm)

Each ordinary recruitment building or Ancestral Workshop can promote **1 living fighter per building level**, limited by the number actually present. The four hero-recruit branches cannot promote anyone.

A promoted fighter gains **0.7 of the max HP recorded when its group initialized**, both as max HP and as current HP, and moves at **1.3 times normal speed**. This is a fixed HP addition, not a new multiplier on every subsequent upgrade. It gives no attack damage.

When a veteran falls, that unit loses the bonus and the group fills the vacancy from its other living fighters. Promotions are also checked after training, building upgrades and sunrise; the perk does not require the fighter to score a kill.
