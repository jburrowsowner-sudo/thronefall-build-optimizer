# Cobbler (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Movement speed | 1.5 | Barracks troops and Golem |

## Hidden mechanics

The Golem gets the speed bonus alongside the four Barracks units. It changes how quickly they reach a fight, not how quickly they attack once there. The ruler and the other heroes are unaffected.

## Craaghelm

The four Barracks recruits, the Automaton and **both** Ancestral Workshop units move at **1.5 times normal speed**. This includes the Ancestor even when it throws its weapon. The other three heroes, ordinary ranged recruits and Spectral Archer do not get this bonus.
