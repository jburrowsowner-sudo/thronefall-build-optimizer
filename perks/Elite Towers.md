# Elite Towers (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Attack damage | Upgraded tower shots and oil |
| Healing per projectile | Upgraded Healing Spire |

**Multiplier:** 1.65 · **Condition:** Built or upgraded; until dawn

## Hidden mechanics

Only a Defense Tower built or upgraded that day gets the bonus. Upgrading the same tower again before dawn does not stack it. The bonus also strengthens that tower’s healing orbs or oil, if it has them.
