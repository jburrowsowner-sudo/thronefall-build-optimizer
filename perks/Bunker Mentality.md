# Bunker Mentality (Perk, Craaghelm)

When a wall segment or gate falls, it pushes living ground enemies on its **facing side**. There is no blast damage. A wide piece uses the same push as a narrow one.

The strip starts at the piece and extends 7 units outward, 12 units across. At least one enemy in the strip must be within 7 units to trigger the push. Direction fans from straight out in the middle to 25° at either edge. The force limit is 20; actual travel depends on the enemy’s distance curve and the ground it can reach. Flyers and enemies without working knockback movement are not pushed.
