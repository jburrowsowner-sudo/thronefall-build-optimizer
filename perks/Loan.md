# Loan (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starting purse | +7 gold | Start of run | Ruler |
| Enemy bounty | −10 gold | Earliest bounties | Wave gold |

## Hidden mechanics

Repayment comes out of the first enemy groups’ coin drops, in order, until 10 gold has been withheld. It does not deduct a lump sum from your purse or reduce building income.

## Impacted damage dealers

None directly.
