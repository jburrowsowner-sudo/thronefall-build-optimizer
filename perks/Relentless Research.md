# Relentless Research (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research progress | Dawn after building knockout | Blacksmith and Royal Forge |
| Research progress | Dawn after a knockout | Blacksmith |
| Research progress | Dawn after a knockout | Royal Forge |

**Value:** +1 day

## Hidden mechanics

A Blacksmith or Royal Forge knocked out during the night still advances research at dawn. An intact building gets no extra progress. Faster Research shortens the research itself; this perk prevents a missed dawn.

## Impacted damage dealers

None directly.
