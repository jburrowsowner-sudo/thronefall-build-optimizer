# Royal Mint (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily income | +1 gold | Each of tiers 1–3 | Town Center |

## Hidden mechanics

It adds dawn income, not starting gold. The Town Center earns 1 extra gold at tier I, 2 at tier II and 3 at tiers III and IV; the final upgrade adds nothing further.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Town Center](../town-center/Town%20Center.md)

## Craaghelm

The Castle Center adds one **mithril** to its daily income at each enabled tier. The running bonus is +1 / +2 / +3 at tiers I / II / III. There is no starting grant, and it does not produce Energy Cores.
