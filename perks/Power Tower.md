# Power Tower (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remaining cooldown | −2.66 s/s extra | Nearest living Defense Tower, including oil |
| Target refresh interval | 0.2 s | Nearest living Defense Tower |

**Condition:** At night

## Hidden mechanics

The bonus follows the living Defense Tower nearest the ruler at night. It shortens the wait for the next shot or oil pour, not the gap between oil ticks. Projectiles already in flight do not move faster.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md)

## Craaghelm

At night the nearest **living tower** to the ruler drains an extra 2.66 s of attack cooldown per second. Target choice refreshes every 0.2 s. This is extra timer drain, not a damage multiplier.

Any active Defense Tower weapon can qualify, as can the powered Ancient Cannon at Zentrum. A Flame Tower does not burn for longer or tick faster. A Tar Tower’s pour and trap cooldowns are distinct from the pool lifetime, slow interval and burn-hit timer. The portable Engineer Turret has a building tag but no tower tag.
