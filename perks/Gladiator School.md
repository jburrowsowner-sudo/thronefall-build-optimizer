# Gladiator School (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Value | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Respawn interval | 0.57 | — | At night |
| Respawn timer | — | Charges early | All recruits alive at night |

**Target:** Archery Range, Barracks, Heroes’ Quarters

## Hidden mechanics

A standing recruitment building can count down at night even while every recruit is alive. If the timer is already ready when a recruit falls, it can replace that recruit immediately. A knocked-out building pauses the timer.
