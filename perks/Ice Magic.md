# Ice Magic (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speed while slowed | 0.6 | — | — | Slowed enemies |
| Slow duration | 3 | — | — | Slowed enemies |
| Mount contact slow | — | 3 s | While riding | Enemy struck |
| Contact interval | — | 0.85 s | Per enemy | Mount contact |

## Hidden mechanics

Riding into an enemy can slow it even without War Horse and without sprinting. Without War Horse that contact deals no damage or recoil. Reapplying a slow keeps the longer remaining duration; it does not add the two durations.
