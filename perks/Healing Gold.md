# Healing Gold (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing | +50 HP · regeneration · healing received | Collect a coin | Ruler |

## Hidden mechanics

The ruler must pick up a coin. Gold added straight to the purse by Loan or Interest gives no healing. The heal scales with ruler regeneration and healing received, and stops at max HP.
