# Heavy Armor (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Max HP | 3.5 |
| Regeneration | 1.35 |

**Target:** Ruler

## Hidden mechanics

The ruler refills to the increased max HP. Despite the armor, movement at night is unchanged in this build. The regeneration increase also strengthens Healing Gold.

## Impacted damage dealers

- **Max HP:** [Blood Wand](../ruler-weapons/Blood%20Wand.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Regeneration:** [Falchion & Traps](../ruler-weapons/Falchion%20%26%20Traps.md) · [Light Spear](../ruler-weapons/Light%20Spear.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
