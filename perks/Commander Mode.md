# Commander Mode (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attack damage | 1.4 | Army, towers, Fortified Houses, Shrines, Town Center, Barricades |
| Healing per projectile | 1.4 | Support Mage and Healing Spire |
| Ruler damage | 0.4 | Ruler |

## Hidden mechanics

The allied bonus includes Support Mage and Healing Spire healing, Fire Spire oil and Barricade hits. It skips mill shots, Scarecrow shots and wall explosions. The ruler loses damage, not max HP; this is separate from the Town Center’s Commander upgrade.

## Craaghelm

The ruler’s damaging weapon channels run at **0.4 of normal damage**. The Royal Spanner still heals for its usual amount. Allied attacks with the perk hook instead use **1.4 of normal strength**.

That includes recruits, Workshop fighters, Spectral Archer arrows, Castle Center shots, fortified houses, barricade hits and Defense Tower attacks. Bootlegger heals, Siege Engineer repairs and Healing Spire orbs also grow. The engineer’s separate turret, brewery and keg weapons, the Ancient Cannon, fixed map traps and the Explosive Revival blast do not inherit this allied bonus.
