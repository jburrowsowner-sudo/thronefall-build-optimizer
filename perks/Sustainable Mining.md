# Sustainable Mining (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Income | +1 gold | On construction |
| Next harvest | 7 gold | Dawn after mine knockout |

**Target:** Mines

## Hidden mechanics

A knockout resets the mine’s declining income, not its HP. The missed harvest is still lost. On intact nights the payout continues to fall by one gold per harvest, down to one.

## Impacted damage dealers

None directly.
