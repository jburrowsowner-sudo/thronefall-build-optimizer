# Architect's Council (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Town Center unlock tier | −1 tier | Normally requires tier 2 or 3 | Dependent Barricades, Heroes’ Quarters, Defense Towers, Mines and gates |

## Hidden mechanics

Only direct Town Center requirements change. Prerequisite houses, mills and gates are still required. Upgrading an existing building still needs the usual Town Center tier.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Barricades](../walls/Barricades.md) · [Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md) · [Wide Gate](../walls/Wide%20Gate.md)
