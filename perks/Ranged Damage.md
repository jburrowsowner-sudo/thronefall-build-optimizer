# Ranged Damage (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ranged damage | 1.25 | Ranged-scaling attacks |

## Hidden mechanics

A Hunter’s arrow benefits but its knife does not. Fire Archer ground burn and Firewing impact damage inherit the bonus from the shot. Healing Spire and Support Mage orbs do not gain healing from it.

## Craaghelm

Multiplies **ranged-channel damage by 1.25**, on top of ranged research. Slingers, Snipers, Grenadiers, Airship fire, Technician drones and Spectral Archer arrows qualify. Thrown axes and thrown Ancestor weapons do not; those use melee damage.

Castle Center, fortified-house, Brewery and Keg Sentry shots qualify, as do Defense Tower arrows, Flame Tower hits, saw blades, cannon blasts, rail beams and the Ancient Cannon. Royal Spanner throws gain enemy damage, not healing. The Engineer Turret’s crossbow, burning tar, map traps and the Healing Spire do not read ranged damage.
