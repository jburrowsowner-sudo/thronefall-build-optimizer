# Castle Blueprints (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max HP | 1.5 | Defense Towers, walls and gates |

## Hidden mechanics

The HP multiplier applies both to starting HP and to HP added by later upgrades. It covers Defense Towers, walls and gates, but not the Town Center itself.

[Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md) · [Wide Gate](../walls/Wide%20Gate.md) · [Wide Wall](../walls/Wide%20Wall.md)
