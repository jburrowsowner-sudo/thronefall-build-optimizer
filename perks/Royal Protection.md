# Royal Protection (Perk)

## Effects

**Stat:** Current HP, Max HP

**Multiplier:** 2.5 · **Condition:** While following the ruler · **Target:** Recruited troops and heroes

## Hidden mechanics

Taking command scales current and maximum HP together, so a wounded recruit stays at the same fraction of health. Releasing it reverses the scaling. The perk does not reduce the damage number of an incoming hit.

## Craaghelm

Taking command of a recruit scales **both current and maximum HP by 2.5**. Its health fraction stays the same; releasing it reverses the scaling. All ordinary recruits, hero choices and Workshop fighters can qualify. The stationary Spectral Archer and Engineer Turret cannot.

This is not damage reduction: Safety Gear and the gate aura still reduce incoming hits separately. A commanded fighter’s larger maximum HP also matters to Parting Gift.
