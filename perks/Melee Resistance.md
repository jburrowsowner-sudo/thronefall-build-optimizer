# Melee Resistance (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melee damage received | 0.76 | Attacks reduced by melee resistance |

## Hidden mechanics

The incoming attack must use melee resistance. Troops, heroes, buildings and the ruler can benefit, but Pikes’ hit when the ruler crosses into reach and War Horse recoil bypass it. Melee Armor research stacks multiplicatively.

## Craaghelm

An incoming attack assigned to melee resistance is **divided by 1.3** (about 0.76 of the original hit). This can protect the ruler, recruits and destructible buildings. Steam Centurion strikes, Piston Lancer thrusts and Forge Golem blows use this channel. An area hit can also use melee resistance; it need not come from a sword.

The divisor multiplies the matching Blacksmith armor research. Safety Gear and the gate aura act on the resulting hit. Direct damage that bypasses the weapon’s resistance channel is not reduced by this perk.
