# Last Stand (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current HP | Restore to full | Defense Towers |
| Revival | Revive | Knocked-out towers |

**Condition:** Town Center hit at or below 0.5 of max HP; once per night

## Hidden mechanics

The Town Center does not have to cross half HP in one hit: another hit while it is already below half can trigger the perk. It restores all built Defense Towers, including knocked-out ones. It rearms at dusk.
