# Archery Skills (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attack range | 1.6 | Archer shots, Lizard Rider snipe, Scarecrow shot |
| Preferred distance | 1.6 | Archery Range troops and Lizard Rider |
| Movement speed | 1.2 | Archery Range troops and Lizard Rider |
| Projectile speed | 1.6 | Archers, Firewing, Rider snipe, Defense Towers, Fortified Houses, awakened Shrines, Town Center |

## Hidden mechanics

Hunter arrows and Lizard Rider snipes gain range; their melee attacks do not. Scarecrows shoot farther without shooting faster projectiles. Firewing’s projectile travels faster without changing its movement speed, and Support Mage orbs are unchanged.

## Craaghelm

**Craaghelm name:** Firearm Drills

| Recipient | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| Slingers, Snipers, Grenadiers | Multipliers: 1.6 to search and stand-off distance; 1.2 to movement; 1.6 to shot speed. |
| Axe Throwers | Movement multiplier 1.2; thrown-axe speed multiplier 1.6. Effective attack distances stay 24 / 3.5. |
| Spectral Archer / Keg Sentry | Both search 1.6 times as far. The archer also fires faster-travelling arrows. Its movement is disabled; the keg has no projectile-speed bonus. |
| Other moving shots | Travel speed multiplier 1.6, not fire rate: Bootlegger heals, Airship fire, Technician drones, Siege Engineer repairs, thrown Ancestor axes, Castle Center and fortified-house shots, arrows, cannon shells and healing orbs from Defense Towers. |

This does not extend the ruler’s firearms, the engineer’s deployed crossbow, flame or rail-beam length, or a healer’s search. The Saw Blade Tower uses its own outward and return speeds.
