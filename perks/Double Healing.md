# Double Healing (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simultaneous healing targets | +1 | Healing Spire |

## Hidden mechanics

A Healing Spire can select an extra wounded ally. It does not double the healing per orb or guarantee two hits on one ally. Support Mages do not gain another orb.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md)

## Craaghelm

The Healing Spire gains **one extra healing projectile** and can select another wounded, living, non-building ally. The ruler, all recruit types and the Workshop fighters qualify. It does not double a single orb’s healing or let a Bootlegger or Siege Engineer fire an extra shot. Neither the engineer’s turret nor the unregistered Spectral Archer qualifies as an orb target.
