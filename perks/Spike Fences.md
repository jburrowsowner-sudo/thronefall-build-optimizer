# Spike Fences (Perk, Craaghelm)

Each **nonlethal, attributed hit** on a fitted building returns **2 damage** to a melee-tagged enemy. The attacker’s tag matters, not how far away it stood. The return is flat: a harder hit does not produce stronger spikes.

Walls and gates qualify, but so do the Castle Center, Defense Towers, houses, recruitment and research buildings, breweries and kegs. Later layouts also fit the Ancestral Workshop, Energy Poles, tunnel entrances and Ancient Cannon. Barricades, drawbridges and the engineer’s portable turret have no spike component.

The final blow does not reflect: the building’s HP has already dropped to zero when it checks the hit. Damage with no identified attacker or from a non-melee enemy returns nothing.
