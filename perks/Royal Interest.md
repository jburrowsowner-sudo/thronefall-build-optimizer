# Royal Interest (Perk, Craaghelm)

At dusk the ruler earns **1 mithril per complete 5 already in the purse**, up to 20. Loose pickups and energy cores do not count.

| Mithril held | Interest |
| --- | --- |
| 0–4 | 0 |
| 5–9 | 1 |
| 95–99 | 19 |
| 100 or more | 20 |

Round down before applying the cap: holding 99 pays 19, not 20.
