# Elite Warriors (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Max HP | 1.5 | Recruited troops and heroes |

## Hidden mechanics

Heroes gain the extra HP as well as ordinary recruits. Attack damage stays the same, and the perk does not slow nighttime replacements in this build.

## Craaghelm

All eight ordinary recruits, four heroes and both Ancestral Workshop units gain **1.5 times normal max HP**. An Automaton with 360 max HP reaches 540. The ruler and Engineer Turret do not benefit. The Spectral Archer has no working health pool, even though its prefab carries a max-HP modifier.

Night replacement time is unchanged (training factor 1). The perk does not multiply attack strength.
