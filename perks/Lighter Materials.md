# Lighter Materials (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto-attack cooldown | 0.67 | Equipped ruler weapon |

## Hidden mechanics

It shortens the automatic attack’s cooldown, not the active ability’s. For Falchion & Traps, that means the pause after the third strike, not the gaps between strikes in the combo.
