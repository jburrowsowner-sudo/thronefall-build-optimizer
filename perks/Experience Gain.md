## Hidden mechanics

Each building counts its own successful nighttime revivals. Only the unit just revived gets the new bonus. The 19th revival reaches three times the normal stat; later revivals stay at that cap. Dusk clears both the count and the bonuses. Dawn and Ring of Resurrection revivals do not count.

## Craaghelm

Each recruitment group tracks its own successful **nighttime replacements**. Its 1.06 multiplier compounds per replacement, reaching the cap of 3 on the 19th. Only the unit just returned receives the current multiplier to max/current HP and automatic attack strength. A Bootlegger’s heal and Siege Engineer’s repair scale with their attacks.

Both Ancestral Workshop branches and all hero choices use this rule. The Spectral Archer and Engineer Turret are not trained replacements. Dusk clears the count and strips prior bonuses; dawn or Ring revivals do not advance it. Call to Arms shortens the wait between eligible replacements, not the number required to reach the cap.
