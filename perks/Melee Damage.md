## Hidden mechanics

The attack matters more than the unit’s name. Hunter knives, Lizard Rider bites, Fire Spire oil and wall blasts use melee damage. The perk multiplies Blacksmith melee research rather than adding to it.

## Craaghelm

Multiplies **melee-channel damage by 1.35**, on top of melee research. Both Axe Thrower weapons and both Ancestor weapons use the melee channel, even at range.

This also covers the four Barracks recruits’ damaging strikes, Automaton, Mad Berserker, barricade contacts, Explosive Brew, the Dwarven Axe and Keg and Hammer attacks, and the Royal Spanner’s damaging aura ticks. It does not improve Bootlegger healing, spanner healing, Parting Gift or Spike Fences.
