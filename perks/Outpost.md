## Hidden mechanics

If either of the two towers is already built, it counts toward the limit. Outpost does not build a replacement.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md)

## Craaghelm

Building the Castle Center selects its **two farthest directly dependent tower plots**, regardless of their normal reveal tier. Each unbuilt candidate becomes a level I Defense Tower. If either is already built, that place is spent; no third plot substitutes. At Schattengrund the candidates are the southwest and northwest outpost towers, not the central cluster.
