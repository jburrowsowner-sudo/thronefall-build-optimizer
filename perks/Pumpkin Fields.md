# Pumpkin Fields (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max HP | 5 | — | — | Fields |
| Daily income | — | +1 gold | On construction | Mills |

## Hidden mechanics

The fields receive the HP, but the mill receives the extra dawn income. Adding more fields does not multiply the income bonus, and later mill upgrades do not add another bonus coin.
