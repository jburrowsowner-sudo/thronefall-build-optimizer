# Arcane Towers (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shot damage | 1.33 | Defense Tower projectiles |
| Healing per projectile | 1.33 | Healing Spire |
| Shot range | 1.2 | Defense Tower projectiles |

## Hidden mechanics

The bonus follows the Defense Tower’s main shot as it upgrades. It increases Healing Spire healing and Fire Spire impact damage, but not the damage or radius of ground oil. Shrines, Fortified Houses and the Town Center get neither bonus.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md)

## Craaghelm

Defense Tower attack strength is multiplied by **1.33**, including Healing Spire healing; maximum acquisition range by **1.2**. The specialist towers do not all use “range” the same way:

| Attack | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Saw Blade / Cannon | Shot or splash strength and target search; Cannon keeps its minimum range. |
| Railgun | Target search and beam hits; beam length and decay per target do not change. |
| Flame / Tar | Flame trigger range and damage ticks; Tar acquisition and burning-pool hits. Flame geometry, tar footprint and slow strength do not expand. |
| Ancient Cannon | Search reaches 120. Ordinary shots get the damage multiplier until a Crystal Dragon phase writes its own multiplier (2 or 0.5). |

This does not strengthen the Siege Engineer’s separate turret or standalone map traps.
