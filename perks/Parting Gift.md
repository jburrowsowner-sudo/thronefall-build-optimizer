# Parting Gift (Perk, Craaghelm)

When a recruit falls, its **recorded attacker** takes damage equal to **0.4 of the victim’s current maximum HP**. A 105-max-HP unit returns 42 damage whether it had 1 or 105 HP before the final hit.

This is a direct hit on that attacker, not an explosion, a fraction of the incoming blow or damage from the ruler’s weapon. An unattributed knockout cannot return damage. The ruler and structures do not retaliate. A Spectral Archer disappearing with its tower’s timer does not die or trigger retaliation.
