# Ancient Shrines (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awakening XP | 2 | — | 350 → 700 XP | Shrines |
| Shot damage | 1.5 | — | After awakening | Shrines |
| Awakening threshold | — | 700 XP | With Ancient Shrines | Unawakened shrines |
| Bolt damage | 1.5 | — | With Ancient Shrines | Awakened shrines |

## Hidden mechanics

The perk builds the shrines but does not awaken them. They need XP from nearby deaths before they can shoot or earn income. Once a shrine awakens, the stronger bolt stacks with the bonus from other awakened shrines.
