# Lead by Example (Perk, Craaghelm)

At night, stay within **10 units** of a recruit to multiply its automatic attack strength by **1.3**. Orders are not required. The unit checks every 0.5 s.

Both Bootlegger attacks qualify, including the heal. So do both adaptive weapons of Axe Throwers and Ancestors, Technician drones and Siege Engineer repairs. The engineer’s separately deployed turret does not inherit the bonus. The Spectral Archer’s disabled HP never passes the living-unit check.

The unit snapshots its own attack multipliers two seconds after starting. Leaving range or being knocked out restores those saved values, not the current value divided by 1.3. Dawn stops the range checks; it does not itself restore a saved value.
