# Safety Gear (Perk, Craaghelm)

The ruler and recruited units gain three distinct protections:

| Protection | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Incoming positive damage | 0.85 of normal. Its reduction adds to Garrisoned Gates rather than multiplying. |
| Movement lost to a slow | 0.65 of the normal penalty. A 0.33 speed factor becomes about 0.56, not 0.68. |
| Slow duration | 0.75 of normal. The ruler shortens the incoming duration; a recruit shortens the longer of the incoming and remaining durations. |

The strength reduction also applies to the dedicated attack-recoil and environmental group-hit paths, before the general damage reduction. It is not a blanket reduction to every effect duration. Buildings and the Engineer Turret do not receive the personal armor.
