# Healing Spirits (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing received | 1.65 | Excludes revival | Ruler, troops, heroes and owned buildings |

## Hidden mechanics

The bonus belongs to the recipient, not the healer. It increases regeneration, healing orbs and other heals on eligible living allies, but not revivals or changes to max HP. It cannot heal past max HP.

## Craaghelm

A living player-owned recipient gets **1.65 times normal healing**. The multiplier belongs to the target, not the healer. It scales Hot Springs ticks, Bootlegger heals, Siege Engineer repairs and Royal Spanner healing on targets those effects already accept. A wounded deployed Engineer Turret can be repaired; a Spectral Archer is not a healing target.

Hot Springs alone becomes 2.47 HP each combat tick or 6.6 outside combat. Revivals, scripted refills and max-HP increases bypass ordinary healing.
