# Glass Cannon (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Ruler damage | 1.8 |
| Max HP | 0.2 |

**Target:** Ruler

## Hidden mechanics

War Horse uses ruler damage for both the hit and the recoil, so both rise. Heavy Armor multiplies the reduced max HP rather than adding its bonus back: together they leave the ruler at 0.7 of normal max HP.
