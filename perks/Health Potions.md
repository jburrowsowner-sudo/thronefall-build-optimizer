# Health Potions (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Healing | +5 HP | Every 2 s | Living troops and heroes |

## Hidden mechanics

The heal ticks every two seconds, even if the unit just took damage. Only living troops and heroes receive it. A fallen unit is not revived, and the ruler and buildings are not healed.
