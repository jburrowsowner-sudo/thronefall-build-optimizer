# Timber Scaffolding (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Town Center tier requirement | Removed | Tower upgrades | Defense Towers |

## Hidden mechanics

An existing tower can advance beyond the Town Center’s tier. Its upgrades still happen in order.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Defense Tower](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md)

## Craaghelm

A revealed Defense Tower can advance through its enabled tiers without waiting for the Castle Center to match its level. This opens the ordinary level II and III choices early; it does not reveal a hidden plot, create a level IV or charge the Ancient Cannon. An Energy Core boost is a separate action.
