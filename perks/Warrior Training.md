# Warrior Training (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ruler damage | 2^((night − 1)/108) | Ruler |

## Hidden mechanics

The first night has no bonus. Ruler damage grows in small steps and is still just under double on night 108; it does not double every night. Allied attack strength is unchanged in this build.

## Craaghelm

The ruler begins at normal strength. On later nights the multiplier is **2 raised to (completed nights / total nights on this map)**. Shorter campaigns build the bonus faster. The last night starts just below 2, not at double damage.

All seven weapons can gain damage. Heals are not weapon damage, and allied attacks have a multiplier of 1. This is separate from the Castle Center’s upgrade choices.
