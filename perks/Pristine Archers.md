# Pristine Archers (Perk)

## Effects

**Stat:** Max HP, Attack damage

**Multiplier:** 1.75 · **Target:** Archery Range troops

## Hidden mechanics

Both Hunter attacks gain damage, including the knife. The bonus is tied to the four Archery Range recruits; it does not extend to the ruler’s bow, ranged heroes, towers or Scarecrows.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Crossbowmen](../archery-range/Crossbowmen.md) · [Fire Archers](../archery-range/Fire%20Archers.md) · [Hunters](../archery-range/Hunters.md) · [Longbow Archers](../archery-range/Longbow%20Archers.md)

## Craaghelm

Slingers, Snipers, Axe Throwers and Grenadiers deal **1.75 times normal attack damage**. Both the thrown axe and close strike qualify; the grenade passes the multiplier to its explosion. The Spectral Archer also gains the shot bonus.

Only **Axe Throwers** gain max HP: 45 becomes 78.75. The other three recruits and the Spectral Archer gain no usable health.
