# Explosive Walls (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knockout blast damage | 30 HP | Tier 1 |
| Knockout blast damage | 60 HP | Tiers 2–3 |
| Blast radius | 8.5 units | On knockout |

**Target:** Walls and gates

## Hidden mechanics

Each knocked-out wall or gate piece can explode, so overlapping blasts from different pieces can hit the same enemy. Tier II replaces the weaker blast rather than adding a second one. Melee Attack strengthens it; ruler damage does not.
