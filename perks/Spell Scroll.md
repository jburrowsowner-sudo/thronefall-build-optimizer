# Spell Scroll (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ability cooldown | 0.65 | Equipped ruler weapon; dagger maximum cooldown |

## Hidden mechanics

Only the active cooldown changes. For Bow & Dagger it scales the maximum stab cooldown; the target’s HP before the stab still sets the actual wait. It does not speed up automatic arrows.
