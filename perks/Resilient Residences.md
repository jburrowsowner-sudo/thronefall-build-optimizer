# Resilient Residences (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Multiplier | Value | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Max HP | 2 | — | — |
| Harvest | — | Retained | After house knockout |

**Target:** Houses

## Hidden mechanics

A house can still be knocked out and keep its dawn harvest. Its HP bonus multiplies the Fortified Houses HP bonus; the two do not add together.

## Impacted damage dealers

[House](../economy/House.md)

## Craaghelm

Houses **and Energy Poles** have 2 times normal HP. A knockout no longer costs the next harvest: the House keeps its mithril, and the pole keeps its Energy Cores. This protects the payout; it does not keep a building standing or give an extra harvest on an intact night.
