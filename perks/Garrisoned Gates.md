# Garrisoned Gates (Perk, Craaghelm)

Stand near an intact gate to take **0.8 of normal damage**. This protects the ruler, ordinary recruits, heroes and Workshop fighters—not the gate, Spectral Archer or Engineer Turret.

Every 0.3 s the gate first searches only **5 units** from its centre, then checks a cylinder with radius 10 and half-height 5. The visible 10-unit ring therefore overstates the reach for ordinary units. Leaving, being knocked out or losing the gate removes protection. Overlapping gates do not stack. With Safety Gear, the two together leave 0.65 of incoming damage before other defenses.
