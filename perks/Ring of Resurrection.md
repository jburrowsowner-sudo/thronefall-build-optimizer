# Ring of Resurrection (Perk)

## Effects

| Stat | Value | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revival delay | 2 s | Ruler |
| Current HP | Restore to full | Ruler, troops and heroes |
| Revival | Revive all fallen recruits | Recruited troops and heroes |

**Condition:** First timed ruler revival each night

## Hidden mechanics

The first timed ruler revival also refills wounded surviving recruits, not just fallen ones. Later ruler revivals that night follow the ordinary rules. The Ring rearms after sunrise, and its revivals do not advance Experience Gain.

## Craaghelm

The first **timed** ruler revival after sunrise takes 2 s instead of 11 s. It restores the ruler to full HP and revives or tops up all registered player units: ordinary recruits, heroes and both Workshop units. It skips the separate Engineer Turret and Spectral Archer. Later revivals that night use normal timing. This is not a trained replacement for Experience Gain or Call to Arms.
