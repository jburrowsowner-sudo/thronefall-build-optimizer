# Hot Springs (Perk, Craaghelm)

The ruler and all recruit types recover HP in 1-second ticks. This is healing **per individual**, not a pool divided among survivors.

| Time since combat | HP per tick |
| --- | --- |
| Under 10 s | 1.5 |
| 10 s or more | 4 |

Positive incoming damage or a locally registered attack trigger restarts the combat timer; a hit need not land. The ruler can keep the higher rate while attacking without being hit: the weapon attacks live on separate objects. The Automaton heals even though its training building has no Hot Springs binding. Healing Spirits scales the ticks; ruler self-regeneration bonuses do not. No revival or overheal. Neither the Engineer Turret nor the Spectral Archer gets these ticks.
