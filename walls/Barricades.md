# Barricades (Walls and gates)

Requires Town Center tier 3. No HP.

Deals **15 melee damage** and slows for **7 s** on contact with a fast-moving enemy that is not already slowed.

## Craaghelm

**Maps:** Widerhall, Myrkforst, Brewerk, Grundsteg, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

One level. The controlling barricade becomes available at [Castle Center (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) level I or II; its linked pieces build together and have no HP. An unslowed fast-moving enemy that overlaps a barricade takes **15 melee damage** and is slowed for **7 s**. Contact does not keep dealing damage while the enemy is already slowed. [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm) scales the hit.

The **5**-unit precheck is not a damage circle: the enemy must overlap the box.
