# Dwarven Bridge (Walls and gates, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Widerhall, Lichtspalt, Zentrum

An adjustable crossing, not an income building. The route starts open. Once the [Castle Center (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) is built, use [Mithril (Craaghelm)](../economy/Mithril.md) to open or close the bridge during the day. Each control allows **one change per day**; dawn restores the interaction rather than automatically changing the route.
