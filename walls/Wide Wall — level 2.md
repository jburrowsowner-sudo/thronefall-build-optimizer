# Wide Wall — level 2 (Walls and gates 2)

Town Center tier 3. Upgrades with its gate group.

| HP gained | HP | HP with Blueprints | Explosive Walls damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | 640 | 960 | 60 |

Explosive Walls hits ground and air enemies within 8.5 units of each knocked-out piece. Separate blasts can overlap.

## Craaghelm

**Maps:** Widerhall, Myrkforst, Grundsteg, Zentrum

The final upgrade raises [Wide Wall (Craaghelm)](Wide%20Wall.md#craaghelm) health from **240 to 640** (+400). This section upgrades with its controlling gate and has its own health. It is tagged as a wall that does **not** close the path; the gate supplies the actual passage.
