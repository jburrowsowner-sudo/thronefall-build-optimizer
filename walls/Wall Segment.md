# Wall Segment (Walls and gates)

This section upgrades with its controlling gate and has its own health. It is tagged as a wall that does **not** close the path; the gate supplies the actual passage.

| Level | Health |
| --- | --- |
| I | 240 |
| II | 640 |

A linked wall section cannot be advanced independently. Level II is the last upgrade here.
