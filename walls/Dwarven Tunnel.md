# Dwarven Tunnel (Walls and gates, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Tiefenlift

Build either entrance to open its partner. Each has **30 HP**; losing one also knocks out the other. The ruler, not the army, can travel through.

Move toward the entrance within **3.5** units for **0.1 s**. The ruler emerges on the far side; both ends have a **0.75 s** reuse delay.
