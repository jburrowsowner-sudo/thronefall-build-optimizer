# Wide Gate — level 2 (Walls and gates 2)

Town Center tier 3.

| HP gained | HP | HP with Blueprints | Explosive Walls damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 400 | 640 | 960 | 60 |

Sliding bars replace the tier 1 doors.

Explosive Walls hits ground and air enemies within 8.5 units of each knocked-out piece. Separate blasts can overlap.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Wide Gate](Wide%20Gate.md)

## Craaghelm

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

The final upgrade raises [Wide Gate (Craaghelm)](Wide%20Gate.md#craaghelm) health from **240 to 640** (+400). Gates open for a living ruler or an allied unit away from home within **5** units, and close when the way clears. The opening animation takes **0.3 s**.
