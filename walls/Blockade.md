# Blockade (Walls and gates, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Zentrum

First drop the rubble to close the route; a later interaction removes it and reopens the path. The blocker is invulnerable rather than a wall that enemies can wear down. After either change the control stays busy until dawn, and there is no third toggle. Both stages use [Mithril (Craaghelm)](../economy/Mithril.md).
