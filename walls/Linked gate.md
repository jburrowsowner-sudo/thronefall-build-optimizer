# Linked gate (Walls and gates)

One of these gates comes with each three-piece group. It has its own HP and opens independently, but upgrades with the group.

| Tier | HP | HP with Blueprints | Explosive Walls damage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 240 | 360 | 30 |
| 2 | 640 | 960 | 60 |
| 3 | 3,140 | 4,710 | 60 |

Opens within 5 units for a living ruler or active troop away from home. Clearance 5, check interval 0.33 s, opening time 0.3 s. Tier 1 uses doors; tiers 2 and 3 use bars.

Explosive Walls hits ground and air enemies within 8.5 units of each knocked-out piece. Separate blasts can overlap.

## Craaghelm

**Maps:** Widerhall, Myrkforst, Brewerk, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift

Looks and opens like a gate, but building or upgrading it delegates to its controlling gate. The connected pieces advance together, not as separately purchased defenses. The gate has **240 / 640 HP** at levels I / II. An attached [Wall Segment](Wall%20Segment.md) has its own health and does not close the route.
