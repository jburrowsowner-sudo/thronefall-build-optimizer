# Blessed Weaponry (Royal Forge)

**Active cooldown multiplier: 0.6** per completed selection. Requires 2 successful dawns, or 1 with Faster Research.

Applies to the equipped active. The dagger’s cooldown multiplier ranges from 0.1 to 1 according to the target’s pre-hit HP fraction. Shield and spear buff durations and free trap placements are unchanged.

[Battle Axe](../ruler-weapons/Battle%20Axe.md) · [Blood Wand](../ruler-weapons/Blood%20Wand.md) · [Bow & Dagger](../ruler-weapons/Bow%20%26%20Dagger.md) · [Falchion & Traps](../ruler-weapons/Falchion%20%26%20Traps.md) · [Heavy Sword](../ruler-weapons/Heavy%20Sword.md) · [Light Spear](../ruler-weapons/Light%20Spear.md) · [Lightning Staff](../ruler-weapons/Lightning%20Staff.md) · [Potion Vials](../ruler-weapons/Potion%20Vials.md) · [Shadow Codex](../ruler-weapons/Shadow%20Codex.md)

## Craaghelm

Available at [Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Royal%20Forge.md#craaghelm) I, II or III. **Equipped active cooldown multiplier: 0.6** per completed pick. Requires **2 successful dawns** by default. The multiplier after one / two / three completions is **0.6 / 0.36 / 0.21**.

Changes the equipped ruler skill’s cooldown, not the damage it deals.

[Boost Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Royal%20Forge.md) can shorten an active project on maps with Energy Cores.
