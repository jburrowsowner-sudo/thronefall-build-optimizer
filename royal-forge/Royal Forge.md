# Royal Forge (Royal Forge)

Choose a passive cooldown, active cooldown, health or damage upgrade for the ruler at each tier.

80 HP and no income. Each of the 3 research tiers requires the matching Town Center tier. Repeated selections multiply.

Research advances at dawn unless the Royal Forge was knocked out that night.

## Craaghelm

This **80-HP** building has three research levels. Select one project each time; choices can repeat and completed factors multiply. A project gains one day at each dawn after a night the building survived. New research waits until the current project finishes.

| Research | Per completed pick | Dawns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Lighter Weaponry (Craaghelm)](Lighter%20Weaponry.md#craaghelm) | Equipped passive cooldown: 0.6 | 2 |
| [Additional Armor (Craaghelm)](Additional%20Armor.md#craaghelm) | Max HP and self-regeneration: 1.6 | 2 |
| [Blessed Weaponry (Craaghelm)](Blessed%20Weaponry.md#craaghelm) | Equipped active cooldown: 0.6 | 2 |
| [Sharper Weaponry (Craaghelm)](Sharper%20Weaponry.md#craaghelm) | Ruler-scaled damage: 1.5 | 3 |

[Boost Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Royal%20Forge.md) removes one day from a project already underway.
