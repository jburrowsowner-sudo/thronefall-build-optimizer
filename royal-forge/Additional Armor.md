# Additional Armor (Royal Forge)

**Max HP and self-healing multiplier: 1.6** per completed selection. Requires 2 successful dawns, or 1 with Faster Research.

Refills a living ruler. Base health: 50 HP; regeneration: 5 HP/s after 1 s without HP loss. Light Spear’s active healing and Healing Gold scale with self-healing; axe lifesteal scales with damage dealt.

## Impacted damage dealers

- **Max HP:** [Blood Wand](../ruler-weapons/Blood%20Wand.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Self-healing rate:** [Falchion & Traps](../ruler-weapons/Falchion%20%26%20Traps.md) · [Light Spear](../ruler-weapons/Light%20Spear.md) · [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)
- **Current HP:** [Ruler](../ruler-weapons/Ruler.md)

## Craaghelm

Available at [Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Royal%20Forge.md#craaghelm) I, II or III. **Max HP and self-regeneration multiplier: 1.6** per completed pick. Requires **2 successful dawns** by default. The multiplier after one / two / three completions is **1.6 / 2.56 / 4.09**.

Also fully heals each living ruler on completion. The regeneration multiplier changes the healing rate, not the delay before it starts.

[Boost Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Royal%20Forge.md) can shorten an active project on maps with Energy Cores.
