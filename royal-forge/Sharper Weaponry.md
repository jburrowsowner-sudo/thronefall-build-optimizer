# Sharper Weaponry (Royal Forge)

**Ruler damage multiplier: 1.5** per completed selection. Requires 3 successful dawns, or 2 with Faster Research.

Includes ruler weapon hits, splash, falchion traps, delayed lightning and War Horse damage and recoil. Blood Wand’s repeat-hit bonus applies ruler damage a second time; its active HP trade does not. Excludes Godly Curse, Magic Armor retaliation and Explosive Revival.

## Craaghelm

Available at [Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Royal%20Forge.md#craaghelm) I, II or III. **Ruler-scaled damage multiplier: 1.5** per completed pick. Requires **3 successful dawns** by default. The multiplier after one / two / three completions is **1.5 / 2.25 / 3.37**.

Changes the ruler’s damage factor. An effect that deals fixed damage without using that factor is not amplified.

[Boost Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Royal%20Forge.md) can shorten an active project on maps with Energy Cores.
