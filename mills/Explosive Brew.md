# Explosive Brew (Brewery, Craaghelm)

Choose at [Brewery (Craaghelm)](Brewery.md) level I instead of the other three improvements.

When the Brewery or one of its kegs is knocked out, it deals **30 / 60 / 90** damage to enemies within **8.5**. The parent Brewery’s level decides the blast, even for a keg. [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm) scales it.

## Affects

**by parent Brewery level**

| Affects | Stat | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kegs (Craaghelm)](../economy/Kegs.md) | Death blast damage | 30 / 60 / 90 |
