# Draft Winds (Brewery, Craaghelm)

Choose at [Brewery (Craaghelm)](Brewery.md) level I instead of the other three improvements.

Checks for the nearest blockable enemy projectile within **34.5** every **0.56 / 0.37 / 0.25 s**. It also gives the Brewery and its kegs the ranged-armor tag; that helps against attacks that check that tag, not every ranged hit.

The Brewery now fires at random enemies within **38**. Each shot deals **2** base damage. Damage multipliers: 4 against ranged fighters; 0.5 against ranged armor. A damaging hit can slow movement for **7.5 s**. Its level I / II / III intervals are **1.65 / 1 / 0.5 s**.
