# Keg Sentry (Brewery, Craaghelm)

Choose at [Brewery (Craaghelm)](Brewery.md) level I instead of the other three improvements.

Each built keg gains its own anti-air shot: **25** damage, **3 s** nominal interval, **48** range. It cannot shoot ground units; [Ranged Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Ranged%20Attack.md#craaghelm) scales the shot.
