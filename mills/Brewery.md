# Brewery (Brewery, Craaghelm)

Choose one improvement when the Brewery is built. Levels II and III keep that choice and require the matching [Castle Center (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) level. Level II can reveal more [Kegs (Craaghelm)](../economy/Kegs.md); level III improves income but does not open another row of kegs. Only [Draft Winds (Craaghelm)](Draft%20Winds.md) gives the Brewery a regular shot.

| Level | Health | Mithril per surviving dawn | With Steady Supply |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 30 | 1 | 1 |
| II | 50 | 2 | 3 |
| III | 80 | 6 | 8 |

| First-level choice | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [Steady Supply (Craaghelm)](Steady%20Supply.md) | Extra income on later upgrades |
| [Explosive Brew (Craaghelm)](Explosive%20Brew.md) | Brewery and keg death blasts |
| [Keg Sentry (Craaghelm)](Keg%20Sentry.md) | A flying-target attack per keg |
| [Draft Winds (Craaghelm)](Draft%20Winds.md) | Projectile interception and a slowing Brewery shot |
