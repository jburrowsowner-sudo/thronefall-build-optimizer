# Steady Supply (Brewery, Craaghelm)

Choose at [Brewery (Craaghelm)](Brewery.md) level I instead of the other three improvements.

Adds one extra Mithril to **each future Brewery upgrade**. Income becomes 1 / 3 / 8 rather than 1 / 2 / 6. Keg payouts do not change.

## Affects

**level II and III**

| Affects | Stat | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Brewery (Craaghelm)](Brewery.md) | Mithril per dawn | +1 per subsequent upgrade |
