# Tar Trap (Trap, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Schattengrund, Zentrum

Slows ground enemies inside the tar at night. It reapplies a **0.5 s** slow every **0.5 s**. A [Flame Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Flame%20Tower.md) sweep can ignite the cluster: **5** damage every **0.2 s** for a **3 s** burning phase. Fire does not consume the trap. Unlike a [Tar Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Tar%20Tower.md) pool, this is a fixed map defense.

One level, **30 HP**. The cluster shares a target list: overlapping parts are not independent damage multipliers.
