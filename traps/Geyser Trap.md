# Geyser Trap (Trap, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Grundsteg

After an enemy enters a trigger, the first strike waits **1 s**. As long as enemies stay in the cluster, it strikes them every **3 s**. Steam hits both ground and air: **10** damage on the ground, **15** against a flying enemy.

One level, **30 HP**. The cluster shares a target list: overlapping parts are not independent damage multipliers.
