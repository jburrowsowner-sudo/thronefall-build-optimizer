# Spike Trap (Trap, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

After an enemy enters a trigger, the first strike waits **0.25 s**. As long as enemies stay in the cluster, it strikes them every **2.5 s**. The crystal spikes deal **30** per contact-area hit; the trap is placed along ground routes.

One level, **30 HP**. The cluster shares a target list: overlapping parts are not independent damage multipliers.
