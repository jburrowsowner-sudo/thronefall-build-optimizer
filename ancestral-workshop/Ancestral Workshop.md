# Ancestral Workshop (Recruitment building, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Choose one type for this plot: [Ancestor (Craaghelm)](Ancestor.md), [Mad Berserker (Craaghelm)](Mad%20Berserker.md). The first level opens the Workshop but summons no one. Each following level uses [Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](../economy/Energy%20Cores.md) to add one warrior of the **original** type; level IV is the limit.

| Level | Building health | Summoned warriors | Seconds per replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 50 | 0 | — |
| II | 50 | 1 | 20 |
| III | 50 | 2 | 20 |
| IV | 50 | 3 | 20 |

The building replaces one fallen unit at a time at night; the timer pauses while the building is knocked out.
