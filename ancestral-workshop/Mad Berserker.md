# Mad Berserker (Ancestral Workshop, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Choose this spirit at [Ancestral Workshop (Craaghelm)](Ancestral%20Workshop.md) tier 1. Tiers 2–4 field 1 / 2 / 3; more spirits do not increase damage per spirit. **HP per unit:** 48. **Movement speed:** 4.

| Action | Amount per target | Range | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ground strike | 33 | 4.5 | 3 s |

Prioritizes large ground enemies, then other ground enemies; within each group it picks the lowest remaining HP fraction, even if everyone is healthy. If a surviving victim has 0.15 of its max HP or less after the strike, a separate execution hit deals its remaining HP. The threshold is not increased by attack damage; bosses are not excluded.

**Attack research:** [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm).
