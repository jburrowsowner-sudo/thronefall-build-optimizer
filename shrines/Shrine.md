# Shrine (Shrine)

Each shrine has **777 HP** and awakens at **350 XP**.

Each built shrine within **20** of a death receives the unit’s full original max HP as XP, before difficulty and elite bonuses. Knockouts give none.

Awakened shrines earn **2 gold per eligible dawn**. Their ranged bolt hits the nearest ground or flying enemy in **20** range. Base interval **0.33 s** (range **0.29–0.36 s**). Each other awakened shrine multiplies bolt damage by **1.2** regardless of distance.

| Awakened shrines | Damage per bolt |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 3.5 |
| 2 | 4.2 |
| 3 | 5.04 |
| 4 | 6.04 |
| 5 | 7.25 |
| 6 | 8.70 |

**Awakened shrine bolt:** projectile speed 40 units/s; maximum chase distance 10,000.
