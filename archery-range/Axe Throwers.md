# Axe Throwers (Archery Range, Craaghelm)

[Marksman's Hall (Craaghelm)](Marksman's%20Hall.md) fields **4 / 8 / 12** at tiers 1–3. **HP per unit:** 45. **Movement speed:** 6. Upgrades add fighters, not damage per fighter.

| Action | Amount per target | Range | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ground strike | 2.73 | 3.5 | 0.5 s |
| Thrown axe vs. flyer | 1.36 | 24 | 1 s |

Looks for the nearest ground or flying target within 35, then closes to the reach of the matching attack. The two attacks share a cooldown. A flying monster takes 7 from the thrown axe before armor. Thrown damage multipliers: 0.5 against ranged armor and 1.33 against ranged vulnerability.

[Firearm Drills (Craaghelm)](../perks/Archery%20Skills.md#craaghelm) does not extend either attack’s reach.

**Defense:** Ranged vulnerability. For example, the damage multiplier on a [Bombard (Craaghelm)](../enemies/Bombard.md) direct hit is 1.5. Other attacks can use a different multiplier.

**Attack research:** [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm) affects both weapons, including the thrown axe.
