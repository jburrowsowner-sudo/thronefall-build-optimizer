Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. Levels 1–2 have the same per-unit stats.

**Walk speed:** 3.5. **Stop distance:** 24.

**Pursuit:** range 55.86; any enemy.

## Attacks

**Fire Archer arrow:** range 25; siege → ground enemies → any enemy. Interval: 3 s (range 2.7–3.3 s). Projectile speed: 18. Target check: 0.7 s.

Arrows damage flyers directly; ground impacts instead burn non-flying enemies within 3.25. The first tick is immediate. If it hits, six more follow at 0.5 s intervals.
