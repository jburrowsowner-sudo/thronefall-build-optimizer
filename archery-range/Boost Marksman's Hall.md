# Boost Marksman's Hall (Building boost, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Power the finished [Marksman's Hall (Craaghelm)](Marksman's%20Hall.md) with [Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](../economy/Energy%20Cores.md) during the day. Until dawn, the active type’s replacement speed is multiplied by **1.5** (4 s instead of 6 s per replacement). This does not add unit slots or attack damage.
