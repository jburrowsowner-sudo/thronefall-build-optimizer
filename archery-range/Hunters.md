## Attacks

**Hunter arrow:** monsters within 35 → any enemy within 25. Interval: 1.4 s (range 1.26–1.54 s). Projectile speed: 20. Target check: 0.7 s.

**Hunter knife:** range 3.5; ground monsters → ground enemies. Interval: 0.5 s (range 0.45–0.55 s). Target check: 0.4 s.

Arrow and knife attack independently. Archery Skills extends the arrow’s reach only.
