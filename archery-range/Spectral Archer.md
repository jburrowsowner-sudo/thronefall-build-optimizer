# Spectral Archer (Archery Range, Craaghelm)

[Sentry Spirit (Craaghelm)](../perks/Sentry%20Spirit.md) leaves this archer at a fallen tower. It cannot move and does not register as an ordinary enemy target. It disappears after 30 s, or earlier if the tower revives. A tower saved by [Blessing (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Blessing.md) does not summon an archer on that knockout.

| Action | Amount per target | Range | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spectral arrow | 7 | 20 | 1 s (0.9–1.1) |

Shoots the nearest visible enemy, ground or air. Each arrow deals 4.2 to a target with ranged armor.

**Attack research:** [Ranged Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Ranged%20Attack.md#craaghelm).
