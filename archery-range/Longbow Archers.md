# Longbow Archers (Archery Range)

| Attack | HP | Damage | Cooldown | DPS | Type | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Longbow arrow | 25 | 4 | 2 | 2 | Ranged | 50, 40 |

Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. Levels 1–2 have the same per-unit stats.

**Walk speed:** 9. **Stop distance:** 34.

**Pursuit:** range 65; any enemy.

## Attacks

**Longbow arrow:** flyers within 50 → any enemy within 40. Interval: 2 s (range 1.8–2.2 s). Projectile speed: 33. Target check: 0.7 s.

Ranged armor does not reduce Longbow damage.
