# Marksman's Hall (Recruitment building, Craaghelm)

Choose one type for this plot: [Slingers (Craaghelm)](Slingers.md), [Snipers (Craaghelm)](Snipers.md), [Axe Throwers (Craaghelm)](Axe%20Throwers.md), [Grenadiers (Craaghelm)](Grenadiers.md). Each upgrade adds four more of that type. Levels II and III require the corresponding [Castle Center (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) level; there is no fourth level here.

| Level | Building health | Unit capacity | Seconds per replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 30 | 4 | 10 |
| II | 40 | 8 | 6 |
| III | 50 | 12 | 6 |

The building replaces one fallen unit at a time at night; the timer pauses while the building is knocked out. At full level, [Boost Marksman's Hall (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Marksman's%20Hall.md) can speed this up on maps with Energy Cores.
