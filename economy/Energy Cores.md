# Energy Cores (Economy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Energy Cores are a second currency, not extra [Mithril (Craaghelm)](Mithril.md). A surviving [Energy Pole (Craaghelm)](Energy%20Pole.md) pays **1 / 3** at levels I / II. Core slots draw only from the core balance.

Use them to accelerate a current [Blacksmith (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Blacksmith.md#craaghelm) or [Royal Forge (Craaghelm)](../royal-forge/Royal%20Forge.md#craaghelm) project, temporarily power a finished [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) or recruiting building, add warriors after founding an [Ancestral Workshop (Craaghelm)](../ancestral-workshop/Ancestral%20Workshop.md), or charge the [Ancient Cannon (Craaghelm)](../towers/Ancient%20Cannon.md) for a night.
