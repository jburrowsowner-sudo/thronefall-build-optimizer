# Energy Pole (Economy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

A surviving pole produces [Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](Energy%20Cores.md), not [Mithril (Craaghelm)](Mithril.md). Its second level is the last; upgrading does not give it a weapon.

| Level | Health | Energy Cores per dawn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 20 | 1 |
| II | 30 | 3 |

It counts as a House for [Builder's Guild (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Builder's%20Guild.md#craaghelm): an eligible level I pole can receive the automatic upgrade instead of an ordinary [House (Craaghelm)](House.md#craaghelm).
