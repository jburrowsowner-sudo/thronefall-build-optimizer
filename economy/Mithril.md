# Mithril (Economy, Craaghelm)

Mithril is the kingdom’s ordinary spending money. [House (Craaghelm)](House.md#craaghelm), [Brewery (Craaghelm)](../mills/Brewery.md) and built [Kegs (Craaghelm)](Kegs.md) contribute to this balance at dawn if they were not knocked out during the night. A building normally pays once per day; adding another keg adds another income source.

[Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](Energy%20Cores.md) use a separate balance. Having more Mithril cannot fill an Energy Core slot.
