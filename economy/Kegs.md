# Kegs (Economy, Craaghelm)

Each keg has **10 HP**, produces **1 [Mithril (Craaghelm)](Mithril.md) per surviving dawn**, and has no further level. Its [Brewery (Craaghelm)](../mills/Brewery.md) must reach level I or II, depending on the keg. The keg must then be built separately.

The parent’s choice applies here: [Explosive Brew (Craaghelm)](../mills/Explosive%20Brew.md) adds a death blast, [Keg Sentry (Craaghelm)](../mills/Keg%20Sentry.md) adds an anti-air shot, and [Draft Winds (Craaghelm)](../mills/Draft%20Winds.md) gives ranged armor. [Steady Supply (Craaghelm)](../mills/Steady%20Supply.md) raises only the Brewery’s income.
