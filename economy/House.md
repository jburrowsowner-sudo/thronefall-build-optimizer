## Craaghelm

A surviving House pays [Mithril (Craaghelm)](Mithril.md) at dawn. Level II is its last upgrade here.

| House level | Health | Mithril per dawn | With Fortified Houses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 20 | 1 | 8 damage / 5 s |
| II | 30 | 2 | 8 damage / 1.66 s |

[Fortified Houses (Craaghelm)](../perks/Fortified%20Houses.md#craaghelm) adds a shot at range 34; without it, the House does not attack. [Builder's Guild (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Builder's%20Guild.md#craaghelm) can provide [House — level 2 (Craaghelm)](House%20%E2%80%94%20level%202.md#craaghelm) automatically.
