# The Scarab (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 250 HP · **Speed:** 3.5 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.15 units/s

**Traits:** monster, large.

**Sweep:** 2 direct + 5 area damage; range 4.2; base cooldown 1 s (0.9–1.1 s). Hit box: 9.28 by 2.6 by 8.82 units (width by height by length). Damage multipliers: buildings 0.3.

The primary target can take both the direct hit and the sweep. Other defenders in the box take only the sweep.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
