# Quarry Drone (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 100 HP · **Speed:** 5 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.65 units/s

**Traits:** flying, siege weapon, fast-moving.

**Shot:** 15 damage; range 15; base cooldown 1 s (0.9–1.1 s). Projectile speed: 15 units/s. Damage multipliers: the ruler 0.75; ranged armor 0.4; ranged-vulnerable targets 1.5.

Targets non-wall buildings first, then the ruler. At 0.5 of max HP or less, it stops firing, loses its flying status and lands. While grounded it heals 20 HP every 2 s. It takes off as soon as its health rises above 50 HP; it does not wait for full health.
