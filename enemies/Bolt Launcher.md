# Bolt Launcher (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 40 HP · **Speed:** 4 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.32 units/s

**Traits:** tower-vulnerable, humanoid.

**Heavy bolt:** 15 damage; range 15; base cooldown 1.6 s (1.44–1.76 s). Projectile speed: 30 units/s. Damage multipliers: the ruler 0.75; buildings 0.15; ranged armor 0.4; ranged-vulnerable targets 1.5.

Uses its first attack to lock on, then fires on subsequent attacks. The lock breaks if the target dies or moves beyond 17 units. Buildings do not show the warning line.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 3.2 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 15.75 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 16.
