# Crossbow Turret (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 20 HP · **Speed:** 5 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.65 units/s

**Traits:** tower-vulnerable, humanoid.

**Bolt:** 2.5 damage; range 20; base cooldown 2 s (1.8–2.2 s). Projectile speed: 20 units/s. Damage multipliers: the ruler 0.8; buildings 0.5; ranged armor 0.4; ranged-vulnerable targets 1.5.

Fires first at flying defenders. A [Clockwork Oliphaunt (Craaghelm)](Clockwork%20Oliphaunt.md) releases 3 crossbow riders when it dies; each fights with the HP, range and damage shown here.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 3.2 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 15.75 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 16.
