# The Scrapper (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Grundsteg

**Health:** 15,000 HP · **Speed:** 6 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.98 units/s

**Traits:** tower-vulnerable, boss.

The Scrapper grows each time 10 of your defenders fall within 50 units, up to 3 times. Destroyed buildings and ruler knockouts also count; the Scrapper need not deal the last hit. Each growth raises maximum HP and heals up to the new cap.

| Stage | Max HP | Heal on growth | Speed | Base shot interval (s) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 15,000 | 0 | 6 | 0.5 |
| 2 | 20,000 | 5,000 | 5.4 | 0.55 |
| 3 | 25,000 | 10,000 | 4.8 | 0.62 |
| 4 | 30,000 | 15,000 | 4.2 | 0.83 |

**Grenade:** prioritizes the ruler, then economic buildings. Range 15; 8-unit ground blast. Shot intervals range from 0.9 to 1.1 times the stage’s base interval.

| Stage | Blast damage | Against towers | Against walls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 | 12 | 30 |
| 2 | 5 | 12 | 30 |
| 3 | 6 | 10.5 | 28 |
| 4 | 7 | 12 | 32 |

**Growth burst:** pushes ground troops back with up to 20 force and deals 10 damage within 25 units. Buildings are unaffected. Dropping through 0.5 of its current max HP makes the Scrapper jump to another site.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 15.75 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 16.
