# Clockwork Oliphaunt (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift

**Health:** 375 HP · **Speed:** 3.2 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.05 units/s

**Traits:** monster, large.

**Smack:** 1 direct + 3 area damage; range 4.2; base cooldown 1 s (0.9–1.1 s). Hit box: 14.02 by 2.6 by 8.82 units (width by height by length). Against buildings: 1.2 direct damage.

**Mounted crossbow:** 2.5 damage; range 20; base cooldown 0.33 s (0.27–0.38 s). Projectile speed: 20 units/s. Damage multipliers: the ruler 0.8; buildings 0.5; ranged armor 0.4; ranged-vulnerable targets 1.5.

The 3 riders share one mounted firing interval. On death, 3 dismount and attack independently as [Crossbow Turrets (Craaghelm)](Crossbow%20Turret.md), each with 20 HP and a 2 s base shot interval.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
