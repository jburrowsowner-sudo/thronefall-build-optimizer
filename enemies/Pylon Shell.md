# Pylon Shell (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 20 HP · **Speed:** 3 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 0.99 units/s

**Traits:** tower-vulnerable.

**Beam:** 2.5 damage; range 20; base cooldown 6 s (5.4–6.6 s). Projectile speed: 100 units/s. Damage multipliers: the ruler 0.8; buildings 0.5; ranged armor 0.4; ranged-vulnerable targets 1.5.

Prioritizes flying defenders. On contact the beam slows its target for 3 s. The damage is dealt on contact, not once per slow tick.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 15.75 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 16.
