# Bombard (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Health:** 20 HP · **Speed:** 3.8 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.25 units/s

**Cannon shot:** 2.5 damage; range 20; base cooldown 2 s (1.8–2.2 s). Projectile speed: 20 units/s. Damage multipliers: the ruler 0.8; buildings 0.5; ranged armor 0.4; ranged-vulnerable targets 1.5.

Fires first at flying defenders. Destroying the gun releases one [Piston Lancer (Craaghelm)](Piston%20Lancer.md) at its position.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 15.75 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
