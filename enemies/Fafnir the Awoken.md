# Fafnir the Awoken (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Zentrum

**Health:** 40,000 HP · **Speed:** 4 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.32 units/s

**Traits:** boss.

**Crystal phase:** stomps for 10 damage within 10 units every 2 s. At the Town Center it stops stomping and uses a belly smash: 10 damage to troops and 20 to buildings, in a 24 by 8 by 21.34-unit box, on a 3 s base cooldown.

After a crystal-phase jump, the landing hits ground targets within 15 units after 0.25 s: 14 damage to troops or the ruler and 23 to buildings, with up to 15 knockback force.

**Anger phase:** starts at 0.5 of max HP (20,000 HP). Fafnir sheds its crystal, becomes invulnerable during the transition and returns at speed 5. It relocates again at 0.3 and 0.15 of max HP. It seeks buildings within 25 units; once hunting the ruler or Town Center, it pursues a living ruler within 20 units of the Town Center and switches to the Town Center outside that distance.

**Flame breath:** a 110° cone reaching 17 units after a 1 s windup, on a 2 s cooldown. At close range: 20 damage, 40 to ordinary buildings, 200 to walls or towers. The damage multiplier falls to 0.2 at the edge; up to 15 knockback force and 5 s stun.

**Fireball:** when the target is beyond breath range, fires up to 30 units away every 2 s. The direct hit deals 20 damage, 15 to the ruler, 73.5 to a tower and 210 to a wall. A target within 2 units of impact triggers a separate 10-damage blast (radius 10), with knockback up to 15 force within 8 units on the ground.

**Fire trail:** drops a pool after moving more than 10 units from the last, or after more than 5 s stationary. Ground targets within 8 units take 1 damage every 0.5 s, up to 9 ticks. Fafnir also summons 2 [Beetles (Craaghelm)](Beetles.md) every 10 s spent in the anger phase.

**[Ancient Cannon (Craaghelm)](../towers/Ancient%20Cannon.md):** a shot targeting Fafnir in the crystal phase deals 400 direct damage and a 40-damage blast. In the anger phase that falls to 100 direct and 10 blast damage.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
