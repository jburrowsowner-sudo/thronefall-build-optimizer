# Leap Bot (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Widerhall, Myrkforst, Brewerk, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift

**Health:** 60 HP · **Speed:** 7.6 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 2.5 units/s

**Traits:** monster, fast-moving.

**Strike:** 3 damage; range 2.6; base cooldown 0.8 s (0.72–0.88 s). Damage multipliers: buildings 0.2.

Jumps across marked obstacles on its way to economic buildings. Each jump takes 1 s, with a 2 s rest between jumps. Its weapon does not select walls.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
