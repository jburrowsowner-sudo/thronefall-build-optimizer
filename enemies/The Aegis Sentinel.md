# The Aegis Sentinel (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift

**Health:** 200 HP · **Speed:** 4 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.32 units/s

**Traits:** monster, large, knockback-resistant.

Alternates 3 s of shielding with 3 s without it. The 6-unit dome stops blockable projectiles that cross its boundary, not shots fired and resolved inside it.

The Town Center, a reachable economic building or a reachable wall within 5 units can start a 10 s fuse. If the Sentinel survives the countdown, it detonates in a 12-unit radius and dies. The blast deals 5 damage to ground troops or the ruler and 15 to buildings; up to 7 knockback force and 3 s stun.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
