# Overloaded Boiler (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 65 HP · **Speed:** 9 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 2.97 units/s

**Traits:** fast-moving, resists some ruler attacks, monster, siege weapon.

**Strike:** 3.5 damage; range 4; base cooldown 1 s (0.9–1.1 s).

Detonates when a living player building comes within 3.5 units (checked every 0.5 s), or when killed. The 6.5-unit death blast deals 90 to troops, 45 to the ruler, 180 to an ordinary building, 90 to the Town Center and 9 to other enemies. Fire/explosion resistance multiplies the blast damage by 0.33.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
