# Beetles (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 50 HP · **Speed:** 14 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 4.62 units/s

**Traits:** monster, fast-moving.

**Bite:** 6 damage; range 2.8; base cooldown 0.4 s (0.36–0.44 s). Damage multipliers: buildings 0.05.

Pursues nearby ground troops before economic buildings.

[The Assembler (Craaghelm)](The%20Assembler.md) summons 5 beetles at each weapon pickup.

[Fafnir the Awoken (Craaghelm)](Fafnir%20the%20Awoken.md) summons 2 beetles every 10 s in its anger phase.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
