# Steam Burster (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Health:** 100 HP · **Speed:** 3 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 0.99 units/s

**Traits:** monster, large, siege weapon.

Conceals enemies from ranged attacks that need a visible target. Melee attacks can still reach them. The first cloud appears after 2 s, then one every 20 s. The larger death cloud appears only if the Burster dies on-screen.

| Steam cloud | Radius | Duration (s) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | 5 | 5.25 |
| On death | 9 | 10.25 |

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
