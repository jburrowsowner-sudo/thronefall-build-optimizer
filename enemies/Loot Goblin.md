# Loot Goblin (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift

**Health:** 25 HP · **Speed:** 5 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.65 units/s

**Traits:** fast-moving.

Steals instead of attacking. It seeks an economic building, then a house, then the Town Center. After 5 uninterrupted seconds at its target it takes up to 5 mithril from your balance and returns to its entry point at 2.5 units/s. It cannot be knocked back while stealing.

Kill it on the return trip to recover the stolen mithril plus 3. The bonus requires it to have taken at least one mithril.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
