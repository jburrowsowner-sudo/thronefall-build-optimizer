# Gear Grinder (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 180 HP · **Speed:** 15 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 4.95 units/s

**Traits:** fast-moving, siege weapon.

**Rolling blades:** 3.5 area damage; range 5; base cooldown 0.25 s (0.22–0.27 s). Hit box: 5.1 by 4.68 by 11.04 units (width by height by length).

Its blades hit every defender in the box, not just the defender that triggered the attack.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
