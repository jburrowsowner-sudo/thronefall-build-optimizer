# Small Beetles (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

**Health:** 12.5 HP · **Speed:** 15 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 4.95 units/s

**Traits:** monster, fast-moving.

**Bite:** 2.4 damage; range 2.5; base cooldown 0.4 s (0.36–0.44 s). Damage multipliers: buildings 0.05.

Pursues nearby ground troops before economic buildings.
