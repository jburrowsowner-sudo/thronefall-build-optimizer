# Piston Lancer (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Health:** 25 HP · **Speed:** 5 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 1.65 units/s

**Traits:** ranged-vulnerable, humanoid, tower-vulnerable.

**Thrust:** 3.5 damage; range 6; base cooldown 1 s (0.9–1.1 s).

Seeks the ruler first. Moving toward a lancer from outside 4 units triggers a separate hit for 6 damage, or 16 while sprinting. Either slows the ruler for 3 s.

**Dismounted rider:** a destroyed [Bombard (Craaghelm)](Bombard.md) releases one lancer with the same HP, speed, thrust and entry damage. Unlike the marching lancer, the rider can thrust at flying defenders and is not tower-vulnerable: a basic tower hits it for 8 instead of 16.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 8 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 9.31 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 16.
