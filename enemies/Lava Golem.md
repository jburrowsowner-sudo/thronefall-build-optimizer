# Lava Golem (Enemy, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk

**Health:** 40,000 HP · **Speed:** 3 units/s · **Slowed speed:** 2.4 units/s

**Traits:** boss.

The golem retreats at 30,000, 20,000 and 10,000 HP. It is invulnerable during each retreat and waits 5 s before jumping back; it does not refill its health bar.

**Swing:** 20 damage to troops; 40 to buildings and 40 to the ruler. Hit box: 10 by 16 by 10 units. Buildings can trigger the swing at 10 units; troops and the ruler at 5. Base cooldown: 3 s (2.7–3.3 s).

**From 0.75 of max HP:** leaves lava after moving more than 5 units between pools, or after standing for more than 5 s. Each pool hits ground targets within 6 units for 1 damage every 0.5 s, up to 9 times.

**From 0.5 of max HP:** two swings followed by a lava spit for 100 area damage in a 10-unit radius. **From 0.25 of max HP:** the swing and spit damage multiplier is 1.3 (130 per spit); the lava-pool ticks stay the same.

**Base damage received per hit:** [Slinger (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Slingers.md) 4 · [Sniper (Craaghelm)](../archery-range/Snipers.md) 7 · [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](../towers/Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) 8.
