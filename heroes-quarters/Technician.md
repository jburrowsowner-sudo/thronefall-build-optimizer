# Technician (Heroes’ Quarters, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

One hero per [Hero's Quarter (Craaghelm)](Hero's%20Quarter.md). **HP per unit:** 60 / 120 / 240. **Movement speed:** 13. HP and attack amounts are shown in tier 1 / 2 / 3 order.

| Action | Amount per target | Range | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drone: single target | 10 / 20 / 40 | 30 | 0.75 s |

Starts each night with 5 ready drones. Each launch consumes one; building a replacement takes 2 s. Once the stock is gone, construction limits the firing rate. Despite the explosion animation each drone damages only its target, ground or air.

**Attack research:** [Ranged Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Ranged%20Attack.md#craaghelm).
