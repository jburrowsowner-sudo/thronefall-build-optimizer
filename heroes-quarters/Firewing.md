| Firewing fireball | 120 | 16 | 1 | 16 | Ranged | 17 | — |

Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. At levels 1 and 2, HP and attack strength are one quarter and one half of the listed values.

**Walk speed:** 10. **Stop distance:** 14. **Night respawn:** 60 s.

**Pursuit:** ground enemies within 35 → any enemy within 60.

## Attacks

**Firewing fireball:** range 17; ground enemies → any enemy. Interval: 1 s (range 0.9–1.1 s). Projectile speed: 30. Target check: 0.5 s.

Flying. The impact area hits once on ground or air contact, within a radius of 3.25. The primary target can take both hits. Fire and ranged resistance on the target affect the direct hit only.
