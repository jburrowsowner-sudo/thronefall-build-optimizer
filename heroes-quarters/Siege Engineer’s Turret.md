# Siege Engineer’s Turret (Heroes’ Quarters, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

The [Siege Engineer (Craaghelm)](Siege%20Engineer.md) deploys this **50 HP** building. Its HP and shot damage do not increase with the engineer’s tier.

| Action | Amount per target | Range | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crossbow bolt | 10 | 10–35 | 1 s |

Shoots the farthest visible ground or air enemy in range. A close enemy inside the minimum range cannot be targeted. The bolt deals a single hit, with no splash or Blacksmith attack multiplier.
