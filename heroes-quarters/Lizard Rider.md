# Lizard Rider (Heroes’ Quarters)

| Attack | HP | Damage | Cooldown | DPS | Type | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lizard Rider arrow | 240 | 21.6 | 0.5 | 43.2 | Ranged | 30 |
| Lizard Rider bite | 240 | 12.6 | 0.5 | 25.2 | Melee | 5.5 |

Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. At levels 1 and 2, HP and attack strength are one quarter and one half of the listed values.

**Walk speed:** 13. **Stop distance:** 25. **Night respawn:** 60 s.

**Pursuit:** range 65; any enemy.

## Attacks

**Lizard Rider arrow:** range 30; flyers → ranged fighters → any enemy. Interval: 0.5 s (range 0.45–0.55 s). Projectile speed: 44. Target check: 0.5 s.

**Lizard Rider bite:** range 5.5; ground ranged fighters → ground enemies. Interval: 0.5 s (range 0.45–0.55 s). Target check: 0.5 s.

Arrow and bite attack independently. Each picks the lowest HP fraction in its first eligible group. Commander’s hold bonus and Archery Skills’ range bonus apply to the arrow, not the bite.
