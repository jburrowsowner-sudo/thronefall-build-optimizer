# Hero's Quarter (Recruitment building, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Myrkforst, Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Choose one type for this plot: [Automaton (Craaghelm)](Automaton.md), [Airship (Craaghelm)](Airship.md), [Technician (Craaghelm)](Technician.md), [Siege Engineer (Craaghelm)](Siege%20Engineer.md). Upgrades improve the same hero instead of adding more. Level II and III double the hero’s health and own attack amounts each time; this does not double the separate [Siege Engineer’s Turret (Craaghelm)](Siege%20Engineer%E2%80%99s%20Turret.md).

| Level | Building health | Unit capacity | Seconds per replacement | Hero health / own attack multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 50 | 1 | 60 | 1 / 1 |
| II | 70 | 1 | 60 | 2 / 2 |
| III | 90 | 1 | 60 | 4 / 4 |

The building replaces one fallen unit at a time at night; the timer pauses while the building is knocked out. At full level, [Boost Hero's Quarter (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Hero's%20Quarter.md) can speed this up on maps with Energy Cores.
