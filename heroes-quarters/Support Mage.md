Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. At levels 1 and 2, HP and attack strength are one quarter and one half of the listed values.

**Walk speed:** 9. **Stop distance:** 10. **Night respawn:** 60 s.

**Pursuit:** range 65; living allies except healers and buildings.

## Attacks

**Support Mage healing orb:** range 26; wounded allies except healers and buildings. Interval: 0.4 s (range 0.36–0.44 s). Projectile speed: 50.

Heals up to four wounded allies, including the ruler, but not healers or buildings. Blacksmith damage research does not change healing. Archery Skills does not change orb speed.

**Projectile interception:** blocks the nearest magic projectile under 30 units away every 0.33 s, independently of healing.
