# Fire Spire (Tower 3)

Town Center tier 3.

+250 HP. The projectile hits an area at its destination; oil damages ground enemies around the tower. Ranged Attack scales the impact. Melee Attack scales the oil.

Projectile impact: **one hit** in a 3.25 radius, ground or air. The area lasts 4 s but does not hit again. Base area damage 4; spire damage multiplier 0.75 before other tower bonuses.

Oil: 6 damage per tick in a 6.5 radius. Five ticks, 0.3 s apart, starting immediately. Each tick checks which enemies are still in range. A pour recharges in 5 s; an empty tower checks again in 1 s. Fire/explosion resistance reduces each tick to 2.4.

**Projectile chase distance:** 10,000.

| Target | Impact damage multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| Tower-vulnerable | 2 |
| Ranged armor | 0.6 |
| Siege weapon | 1.4 |
| Fire or explosion resistance | 0.5 |
