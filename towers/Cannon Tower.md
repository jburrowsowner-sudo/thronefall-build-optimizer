# Cannon Tower (Tower 3, Craaghelm)

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) III · **+0 HP**; final HP is **250 / 450 / 100 / 175** after Castle Tower / Armored Tower / Flame Tower / Tar Tower respectively.

Fires at the farthest eligible ground enemy **20–60** units away every **6 s**. On landing, the shell creates a short-lived attack that must find an enemy within **2**. Its explosion deals **20** damage (**40** against siege) within radius **10**. It can knock back ground enemies within **5**, up to force **3**. It cannot shoot point-blank.
