# Flame Tower (Tower 2, Craaghelm)

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) II · **+0 HP**; **100 tower HP** after construction.

Replaces arrows with **4** rotating lines. A ground target within **10** starts a **2 s** sweep at **90°/s**; the **6 s** cooldown starts after the sweep. Contact marks a target for **5 s** of **10** damage ticks at **0.3 s** intervals. The lines also ignite a nearby [Tar Tower (Craaghelm)](Tar%20Tower.md) pool or [Tar Trap (Craaghelm)](../traps/Tar%20Trap.md).
