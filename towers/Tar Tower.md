# Tar Tower (Tower 2, Craaghelm)

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) II · **+75 HP**; **175 tower HP** after construction.

Replaces arrows with a tar pool when a ground enemy enters **7** range. The pool lasts **5 s**, then the pour waits **3 s**. While enemies occupy it, it reapplies a **0.5 s** slow every **1 s**. The pour itself does not deal projectile damage.

[Flame Tower (Craaghelm)](Flame%20Tower.md) can ignite it for **3 s**; burning tar deals **5** per hit at **0.5 s** intervals. A level III upgrade replaces the pool.
