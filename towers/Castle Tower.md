# Castle Tower (Tower 2)

Town Center tier 2.

Targets the nearest enemy. Its shorter cooldown and faster projectiles carry over to spire upgrades.

| HP | HP with Blueprints | Damage per hit | Cooldown (s) | Range | Shot speed | DPS per target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 250 | 375 | 8 | 1.2 | 36 | 40 | 6.66 |

Shot interval multiplier: 0.9–1.1. Target recheck: 1 s. Rates are per occupied target slot, before the Bunker bonus.

## Impacted damage dealers

[Defense Tower](Defense%20Tower.md)

## Craaghelm

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) II · **+150 HP**; **250 tower HP** after construction.

Keeps the basic arrow, firing **8** damage at the closest enemy within **36**. The interval falls to **1.2 s** and projectile speed rises to **40**. Target multipliers stack: tower-vulnerable 2, ranged armor 0.6, flying 1.15.

[Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm) keeps the shorter interval; the three other level III weapons have separate attack timers.
