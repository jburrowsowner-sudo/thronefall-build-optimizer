# Bunker Tower (Tower 2)

Town Center tier 2.

Targets a random enemy. Shot cooldown recovery multiplier starts at 1, rises by 0.2 per second of firing to a maximum of 6, and falls by 0.4 per idle second. Reaches full speed after 25 s of firing; resets at dusk. Oil is unaffected.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 175 | 262 | 8 | 2 | 0.33 | 19.8 | 20 | 4 |

Shot interval multiplier: 0.9–1.1. Target recheck: 1 s. Rates are per occupied target slot, before the Bunker bonus.
