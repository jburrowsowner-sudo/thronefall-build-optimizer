# Ancient Cannon (Tower, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Zentrum

Restore the cannon with [Mithril (Craaghelm)](../economy/Mithril.md), then charge it with [Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](../economy/Energy%20Cores.md) during the day. A charge powers the next night only. The cannon has **100 HP** and is separate from the [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) upgrade tree.

| Attack | Unmodified value |
| --- | --- |
| Firing interval / targeting range | 20 s / 5–100 |
| Direct hit | 50 to an enemy; +50 if siege; +150 if a boss |
| Impact around the hit | 20 damage to ground enemies in radius 15; up to 1 s stun |
| [Fafnir the Awoken (Craaghelm)](../enemies/Fafnir%20the%20Awoken.md) damage multiplier | 2 in crystal phase; 0.5 in anger phase |

It picks the farthest eligible boss first (including a flying boss), then a non-fast ground enemy, then another ground enemy. Ordinary flyers are not selected. The direct hit and explosion both use ranged research.
