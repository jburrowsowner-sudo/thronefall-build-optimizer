# Saw Blade Tower (Tower 3, Craaghelm)

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) III · **+350 HP**; final HP is **600 / 800 / 450 / 525** after Castle Tower / Armored Tower / Flame Tower / Tar Tower respectively.

Throws a blade toward the nearest eligible enemy within **30** every **3 s**. It deals **18** per contact, slows for **5 s**, and hovers for **0.8 s** before returning. Enemies can be hit again on the way back. The outward and return base speeds are **70 / 50**. Damage multipliers: 2 against tower-vulnerable targets; 1.15 against flying targets.
