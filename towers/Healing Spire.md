# Healing Spire (Tower 3)

Town Center tier 3.

+750 HP. Heals the wounded ally with the lowest remaining HP fraction, excluding buildings. Base healing 8; base orb speed 30. Multipliers: healing 1.1, cooldown 0.7, orb speed 1.5. Blacksmith attack bonuses do not raise healing.

**Projectile chase distance:** 40.

## Craaghelm

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) III · **+750 HP**; final HP is **1000 / 1200 / 850 / 925** after Castle Tower / Armored Tower / Flame Tower / Tar Tower respectively.

Stops attacking enemies. Each orb heals a wounded non-building ally for **8.8**, choosing the lowest current health fraction within **36**. Normally it fires every **1.4 s**, or **0.84 s** after [Castle Tower (Craaghelm)](Castle%20Tower.md#craaghelm). [Double Healing (Craaghelm)](../perks/Double%20Healing.md#craaghelm) adds one projectile for another eligible target; [Ranged Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Ranged%20Attack.md#craaghelm) does not scale the heal.
