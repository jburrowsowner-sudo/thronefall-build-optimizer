# Boost Defence Tower (Tower boost, Craaghelm)

**Maps:** Brewerk, Lichtspalt, Grundsteg, Schattengrund, Tiefenlift, Zentrum

Power a level III [Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) with [Energy Cores (Craaghelm)](../economy/Energy%20Cores.md) during the day. Its active weapon’s cooldown is multiplied by **0.7** until dawn; damage per hit and range do not change.

| Level III weapon | Normal interval | Boosted interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Saw Blade Tower (Craaghelm)](Saw%20Blade%20Tower.md) | 3 s | 2.1 s |
| [Cannon Tower (Craaghelm)](Cannon%20Tower.md) | 6 s | 4.2 s |
| [Railgun Tower (Craaghelm)](Railgun%20Tower.md) | 3 s | 2.1 s |
| [Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm) | 1.4 s | 0.98 s |

With [Castle Tower (Craaghelm)](Castle%20Tower.md#craaghelm) followed by [Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm), the boosted interval is **0.58 s**.
