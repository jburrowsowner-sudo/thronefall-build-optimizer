## Craaghelm

The basic tower has **100 HP** and fires one **8**-damage arrow at the nearest enemy within **36** every **2 s** (nominal). Target multipliers stack: tower-vulnerable 2, ranged armor 0.6, flying 1.15.

| Upgrade level | Choose one |
| --- | --- |
| II | [Castle Tower (Craaghelm)](Castle%20Tower.md#craaghelm), [Armored Tower (Craaghelm)](Armored%20Tower.md#craaghelm), [Flame Tower (Craaghelm)](Flame%20Tower.md), [Tar Tower (Craaghelm)](Tar%20Tower.md) |
| III | [Saw Blade Tower (Craaghelm)](Saw%20Blade%20Tower.md), [Cannon Tower (Craaghelm)](Cannon%20Tower.md), [Railgun Tower (Craaghelm)](Railgun%20Tower.md), [Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm) |

The tower normally cannot exceed the [Castle Center (Craaghelm)](../town-center/Town%20Center.md#craaghelm) level. All **16** level II and III pairs are possible; health additions stack. Level III replaces the prior attack, except that [Healing Spire (Craaghelm)](Healing%20Spire.md#craaghelm) reuses the ordinary firing mechanism. An [Armored Tower (Craaghelm)](Armored%20Tower.md#craaghelm) keeps its shield. There is no level IV; where available, use [Boost Defence Tower (Craaghelm)](Boost%20Defence%20Tower.md) at level III.
