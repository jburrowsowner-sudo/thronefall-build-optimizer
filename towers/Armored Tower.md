# Armored Tower (Tower 2)

Town Center tier 2.

Targets flyers first, then other enemies, by lowest remaining HP fraction. The first hit restores full HP and grants 6.5 s of invulnerability. The shield recharges at dawn.

| HP | HP with Blueprints | Damage per hit | Cooldown (s) | Range | Shot speed | DPS per target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 450 | 675 | 8 | 2 | 36 | 20 | 4 |

Shot interval multiplier: 0.9–1.1. Target recheck: 1 s. Rates are per occupied target slot, before the Bunker bonus.

## Craaghelm

[Defense Tower (Craaghelm)](Defense%20Tower.md#craaghelm) II · **+350 HP**; **450 tower HP** after construction.

Prioritizes flying enemies, then picks the lowest current health fraction. The arrow remains **8** damage, range **36**, every **2 s**. On the first damage trigger, restores the tower to full health and grants **6.5 s** of invulnerability. The shield becomes available again at dawn and survives the choice of a level III weapon.
