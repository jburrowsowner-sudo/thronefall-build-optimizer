# Shield Bearers (Barracks, Craaghelm)

[Barracks (Craaghelm)](Barracks.md) fields **4 / 8 / 12** at tiers 1–3. **HP per unit:** 105. **Movement speed:** 2. Upgrades add fighters, not damage per fighter.

| Action | Amount per target | Range | Interval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sweep per enemy | 1.65 | 3.5 | 1 s (0.9–1.1) |

Engages ground enemies and sweeps a 3.7-unit radius. On hold position, the bearer takes 0.6 of normal damage and gives the same protection to the ruler and other living troops or heroes within 8. Shield Bearers only get their own hold-position bonus; multiple auras do not stack.

**Defense:** Ranged armor. For example, the damage multiplier on a [Bombard (Craaghelm)](../enemies/Bombard.md) direct hit is 0.4. Other attacks can use a different multiplier.

**Attack research:** [Melee Attack (Craaghelm)](../blacksmith/Melee%20Attack.md#craaghelm).
