Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. Levels 1–2 have the same per-unit stats.

**Walk speed:** 5. **Stop distance:** 3.5.

**Pursuit:** range 40; ground enemies.

## Attacks

**Flail sweep:** range 4; ground enemies. Interval: 1 s (range 0.9–1.1 s). Target check: 0.5 s.

The sweep hits every enemy within 3.63, including nearby flyers, and restores 1 HP to the Flail per enemy hit.
