Level 3, per unit and target. Cooldown in seconds. Levels 1–2 have the same per-unit stats.

**Walk speed:** 6.5. **Stop distance:** 2.

**Pursuit:** ground siege within 26 → ground enemies within 100.

## Defense: fire and explosion resistance

## Attacks

**Berserk strike:** range 3; ground siege → ground enemies. Interval: 0.5 s (range 0.45–0.55 s). Target check: 0.5 s.

**Berserk leap and landing:** range 7–25. Checks every 0.75 s for open ground within 1 of the target; prioritizes siege weapons. Regular strikes pause in flight. One enemy takes melee damage on landing. Invulnerable for the 0.9 s flight and 2 s afterward. Cooldown after landing: 8 s. Cannot leap while following the ruler.
